# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 85/2005/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-30

The pinned NSO instrument `85/2005/NĐ-CP@2005-07-22` now has a direct official
VBPL print/DOC recovery path, complete parallel commercial transcriptions, and

## Established identity and topology

The sources identify Decree 85/2005/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government on
2005-07-07, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled as the
establishment of communes in Núi Thành and Điện Bàn districts, Quảng Nam
province.

Article 1 divides four predecessor communes into ten successors:

| Predecessor | Successor | Area | Population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Tam Mỹ | Tam Mỹ Đông | 1,727 ha | 6,400 |
| Tam Mỹ | Tam Mỹ Tây | 5,104 ha | 5,869 |
| Tam Anh | Tam Anh Bắc | 2,100 ha | 6,409 |
| Tam Anh | Tam Anh Nam | 2,191 ha | 9,316 |
| Điện Nam | Điện Nam Bắc | 814.45 ha | 5,065 |
| Điện Nam | Điện Nam Trung | 767.50 ha | 6,728 |
| Điện Nam | Điện Nam Đông | 842.25 ha | 6,256 |
| Điện Thắng | Điện Thắng Bắc | 357.20 ha | 5,792 |
| Điện Thắng | Điện Thắng Trung | 378.60 ha | 7,260 |
| Điện Thắng | Điện Thắng Nam | 506.20 ha | 6,122 |

The Tam Mỹ and Tam Anh divisions belong to Núi Thành district. The Điện Nam
and Điện Thắng divisions belong to Điện Bàn district. Four predecessor units
become ten successor units, a net increase of six commune-level units.

## Date discrepancy

The three dates must remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2005-07-07`;
- official effective date: `2005-07-30`; and
- pinned NSO registry/effective-event date: `2005-07-22`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. VBPL's
official search metadata and the Ministry of Home Affairs' later official
systematization both give 2005-07-30 as the effective date. The NSO key is
therefore eight days earlier than legal effectiveness. It remains an
observation/registry date rather than being silently rewritten.

## Complete commercial transcriptions

LuatVietnam publishes the complete text:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/linh-vuc-khac/nghi-dinh-85-2005-nd-cp-chinh-phu-29344-d1.html>

It includes the formal identity, recitals, all four divisions, areas,
populations, boundary descriptions, effective and implementation clauses, and
signature block. Its summary gives 2005-07-30 as the effective date. Its signed
PDF and Word downloads are member-gated commercial copies.

Thư Viện Pháp Luật also publishes a complete transcription:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bat-dong-san/Nghi-dinh-85-2005-ND-CP-thanh-lap-xa-thuoc-huyen-Nui-Thanh-Dien-Ban-tinh-Quang-Nam-9281.aspx>

The two copies agree on the four predecessor divisions, ten successors, every
area and population, and the operative clauses. They disagree in two boundary
strings:

- for Tam Anh Bắc's northern boundary, LuatVietnam has `Tam Xuân 2`, while
  TVPL renders `Tam Anh Xuân 2`; and
- for Điện Thắng Nam's eastern boundary, LuatVietnam has `Điện Thắng Trung,
  Điện Nam Bắc`, while TVPL renders `Điện Thắng Trung, Điện Thắng Bắc`.

These look like transcription differences, but this review does not silently
correct either one without the official bytes. The divergence is immaterial to
the four whole-commune split relationships and will be adjudicated when the
official DOC or page can be archived.

Classification: both pages are `secondary_full_text`; neither is an official
enacted artifact. The TVPL URL is now retained in the deterministic secondary
source map.

## Quảng Nam/Đà Nẵng press corroboration

The Quảng Nam newspaper article now served by Báo Đà Nẵng was published on
2016-09-09:

<https://baodanang.vn/bia-khac-ten-liet-si-o-nghia-trang-liet-si-tinh-nhung-sai-sot-can-som-khac-phuc-3102318.html>

The article criticizes obsolete and incomplete commune labels on the
provincial martyrs' cemetery. It lists Tam Mỹ Đông, Tam Mỹ Tây, Tam Anh Bắc,
and Tam Anh Nam among missing Núi Thành labels. It separately names all six
Điện Nam and Điện Thắng successors and expressly states that the Government
created them by Decree 85/2005/NĐ-CP more than ten years earlier.

Classification: `state_press_retrospective_topology_corroboration`. It is
useful independent evidence that the successor roster was implemented and
recognized locally, but it is neither enacted text nor an original attachment.
