# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 29/2006/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `29/2006/NĐ-CP@2006-04-07` now has two complete
commercial legal-database transcriptions, an indexed official VBPL record, an
official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization entry, and contemporaneous
Government press corroboration supplied by the maintainer. It remains
primary-source-open because no complete official page or original attachment
could be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The reviewed sources identify Decree 29/2006/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2006-03-23, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc thành lập phường thuộc thị xã Sơn La, tỉnh Sơn La

The three relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-03-23`;
- official effective date: `2006-04-15`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-04-07`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL index and the Ministry of Home Affairs' 2024 systematization
register give 2006-04-15 as the legal effective date. The NSO snapshot observes
the two retypes eight days earlier. Its date remains an observation key and is
not silently rewritten.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 performs two whole-unit retypes under Sơn La town:

| Predecessor | Successor | Code | Area | Population |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Xã Chiềng Sinh | Phường Chiềng Sinh | `03679` | 2,269 ha | 11,163 |
| Xã Chiềng An | Phường Chiềng An | `03667` | 2,262.50 ha | 4,951 |

Each ward is formed from the entire area and population of the same-named
commune. The decree therefore creates no territorial split and no new roster
identity: both administrative codes survive while the Vietnamese unit label
and type change from `Xã` to `Phường`.

The `soap:2006-04-06->2006-04-07` observation agrees exactly. It contains no
additions and no removals, and its only two same-code changes are:

- `03667`: `Xã Chiềng An` → `Phường Chiềng An`; and
- `03679`: `Xã Chiềng Sinh` → `Phường Chiềng Sinh`.

Both rows change `TenPhuongXa` and `LoaiHinh`, with no parent-code change. The
crosswalk independently links both same-code rows to Decree 29. The raw SOAP
rows render district code `116` with the later label `Thành phố Sơn La`; the
2006 legal parent was `thị xã Sơn La`. This historical current-label echo is
preserved and is not treated as contrary legal topology.

## Official-source recovery boundary

The official VBPL record is indexed at:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=16493>

The public index matches the decree code, title, 2006-03-23 issue date, and
2006-04-15 effective date. The Ministry of Home Affairs' official 2024
systematization volume independently lists the same code, title, issue date,
and effective date:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/HHTDanhMuc/Attachments/633/Tap%20HTH%202024.pdf>

These official indexed representations establish identity and dates, but they
do not satisfy the raw-artifact gate. A direct request to the VBPL full-text
page returned an HTTP 403 challenge. The indexed output did not expose an
exact attachment filename, so no filename is guessed or registered. The
legacy `vbpl.moj.gov.vn` hostname failed DNS resolution.

Fresh exact-code and title searches on the Government legal portal found no
matching Decree 29 record; the code search returned only the unrelated Decree
129/2006/NĐ-CP substring match. A year-bounded Government Gazette API search
returned no publication PDF. No interactive browser was connected for a
browser-mediated recovery attempt.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with raw retrieval blocked. Primary
closure still requires saving the complete official page or an attachment it
actually exposes, recording its source metadata and hash, and verifying its
identity and contents offline.

## Government press corroboration

Báo Điện tử Chính phủ published a contemporaneous report on 2006-03-24:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/thanh-lap-phuong-chieng-sinh-va-chieng-an-thuoc-tinh-son-la-1024944.htm>

The article says the Government issued the decree on 2006-03-23, identifies
both new wards, gives their exact areas and populations, summarizes their
boundaries, names the responsible implementing bodies, and cites Decree
29/2006/NĐ-CP. Classification:
`official_government_press_contemporaneous_enactment_corroboration`.

The article renders Chiềng Sinh's eastern neighbor once as `Chiềng Nhần`, while
both complete legal transcriptions and the same article's northern-boundary
sentence use `Chiềng Ngần`. This is treated as a press typo, not an alternate
administrative unit or legal boundary.

The article is official and contemporaneous, but it is a short news summary,
not the complete enacted instrument. It therefore strengthens identity and
topology without closing primary provenance.
