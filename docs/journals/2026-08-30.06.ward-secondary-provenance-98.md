# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 98/2005/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-30

The pinned NSO instrument `98/2005/NĐ-CP@2005-08-10` now has a complete
indexed official VBPL print view, an exact original-DOC path, a complete
commercial transcription, a second commercial legal-database record, and
contemporaneous and retrospective press corroboration supplied by the
maintainer. It remains primary-source-open because live retrieval of the

saved, hashed, or verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The official record identifies Decree 98/2005/NĐ-CP as issued by the
Government on 2005-07-26, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc thành lập thị xã Tân Hiệp, tỉnh Hậu Giang; thành lập phường, xã
> thuộc thị xã Tân Hiệp

The three relevant dates must remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2005-07-26`;
- official effective date: `2005-08-17`; and
- pinned NSO registry/effective-event date: `2005-08-10`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL record gives 2005-08-17 as the effective date. The NSO snapshot
therefore reflects the new units seven days before legal effectiveness. The
registry date remains an observation key and is not silently rewritten.

The raw NSO title spells the first occurrence as `Tân HIệp`. The official text
uses `Tân Hiệp`. This source-level capitalization discrepancy is preserved
rather than changing the raw legal-index row.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 creates Tân Hiệp town from the whole of Phụng Hiệp township, Đại
Thành commune, and Tân Thành commune, plus parts of Phụng Hiệp and Tân Phước
Hưng communes. The new town has 7,894.93 ha and 61,024 people.

The same article creates four subordinate units:

| Result | Constituent territory | Resulting area | Resulting population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Phường Ngã Bảy | parts of thị trấn Phụng Hiệp, xã Phụng Hiệp, and xã Đại Thành | 472.95 ha | 13,564 |
| Phường Lái Hiếu | parts of thị trấn Phụng Hiệp, xã Phụng Hiệp, and xã Tân Phước Hưng | 816.37 ha | 9,826 |
| Phường Hiệp Thành | parts of thị trấn Phụng Hiệp, xã Đại Thành, and xã Tân Phước Hưng | 1,225.18 ha | 11,049 |
| Xã Hiệp Lợi | part of xã Phụng Hiệp | 1,515.73 ha | 7,986 |

Tân Hiệp consequently has six commune-level units: Ngã Bảy, Lái Hiếu, and
Hiệp Thành wards, plus Hiệp Lợi, Đại Thành, and Tân Thành communes. Đại Thành
and Tân Thành survive as same-identity units under the new district-tier
parent. Phụng Hiệp township is consumed by the three new wards. The decree
therefore produces four additions, one removal, and a net increase of three
commune-level units.

## SOAP reconciliation and later name continuity

The `soap:2005-07-22->2005-08-10` observation contains nine additions, one
removal, and ten same-code parent changes. Decree 98 accounts for four
additions—Ngã Bảy (`31340`), Lái Hiếu (`31341`), Hiệp Thành (`31343`), and
Hiệp Lợi (`31344`)—the removal of Phụng Hiệp township (`31390`), and the
same-code parent moves of Đại Thành (`31411`) and Tân Thành (`31414`). The five
Kiên Giang additions belong to Decree 97, while the other eight same-code
parent changes belong to Decree 96.

The SOAP after-state renders district code `931` with the later current label
`Thành phố Ngã Bảy`; the 2005 legal parent was `thị xã Tân Hiệp`. The removed
Phụng Hiệp row also retains a stale province echo of `Thành phố Cần Thơ` even
though its district and legal event belong to Hậu Giang. These raw source
echoes are preserved and are not treated as contrary legal topology.

The identity chain is:

- Decree 98/2005/NĐ-CP created `thị xã Tân Hiệp` in 2005;
- Decree 124/2006/NĐ-CP later renamed it `thị xã Ngã Bảy`; and
- the district was subsequently elevated to `thành phố Ngã Bảy`.

Decree 98 itself must not be retroactively described as creating Ngã Bảy town
or city.

## Press corroboration

Tin nhanh Chứng khoán published a TTXVN report on 2019-10-04:

<https://www.tinnhanhchungkhoan.vn/hdnd-tinh-hau-giang-quyet-nghi-thanh-lap-tp-nga-bay-post221869.html>

Báo Cần Thơ published the same TTXVN report:

<https://baocantho.com.vn/hdnd-tinh-hau-giang-quyet-nghi-thanh-lap-thanh-pho-nga-bay-a113830.html>

Both say the 2005 instrument created the town under the name Tân Hiệp and that
Decree 124/2006/NĐ-CP renamed it Ngã Bảy. They also preserve the later six-unit
roster: four wards after Hiệp Lợi's later retype, plus Đại Thành and Tân Thành
communes. Because the two pages reproduce the same wire report, they are one
retrospective corroborating account rather than two independent sources.

Báo Sài Gòn Giải Phóng published a contemporaneous report on 2006-07-09:

<https://www.sggp.org.vn/doi-ten-thi-xa-tan-hiep-thanh-thi-xa-nga-bay-post151094.html>

It reports the Hậu Giang People's Council vote to request the rename, identifies
Tân Hiệp as created on 2005-07-26 under Decree 98, and says it was split from
Phụng Hiệp district. Classification:
`state_press_contemporaneous_identity_and_rename_proposal_corroboration`. The
article predates the later Government rename decree and is not evidence that
the rename was already legally effective on publication.
