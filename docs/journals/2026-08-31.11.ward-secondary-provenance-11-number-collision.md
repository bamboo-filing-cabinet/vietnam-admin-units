# Ward Decree 11 provenance: Thanh Hóa–Hà Giang number collision

Date: 2026-08-31

The five supplied URLs identify the later Hà Giang Decree 11/NĐ-CP, not the
open Thanh Hóa instrument represented by `11/NĐ-CP@2009-01-07`. This review
keeps the two legal identities separate by date, adds the correct complete
Thanh Hóa transcription to the open row, and adds the supplied TVPL
transcription only to the already closed Hà Giang row.

The Thanh Hóa row moves from `missing` to `secondary_only`. It remains
primary-source-open because the correct official VBPL page could be rendered
through indexed output but not saved and hash-verified offline.

## The open row is the 2008 Thanh Hóa decree

The correct official record is VBPL Item 12652:

- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12652>
- <https://vbpl.vn/quangninh/Pages/vbpq-print.aspx?ItemID=12652>

The rendered official text identifies Decree 11/NĐ-CP, signed by Prime
Minister Nguyễn Tấn Dũng on `2008-12-23`, concerning Mường Lát and Quan Sơn
districts, Thanh Hóa. The relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2008-12-23`;
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2009-01-07`; and
- official effective date: `2009-01-18`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
repository therefore preserves `2009-01-07` as an observation key and does
not rewrite it as the legal effective date.

The print mirror renders the complete clauses but substitutes `Quảng Ninh`
for the place in the dateline. The central Item 12652 identity, decree title,
number, issue date, signer, and operative clauses agree; the mirror header is
recorded as a rendering artifact and must not be propagated as legal
provenance.

people, and Trung Thượng with 5,217.41 hectares and 2,894 people. Mường Lát
then has nine commune-level units and Quan Sơn has 13.

Crosswalk rows 13 and 14 in the `2009-01-01` to `2010-01-01` workbook match
these two additions by name, code, date, district, and predecessor territory.

One existing derived link is not enacted by this decree: row 3455 assigns the
Lãng Công parent change in Vĩnh Phúc to Decree 11. The adjacent Vĩnh Phúc
parent changes cite Decree 09/NĐ-CP, which created Sông Lô district from Lập
Thạch. The complete Decree 11 text proves row 3455 is an isolated crosswalk
document-code error, not a third Decree 11 component. This source review does
not mutate the archived workbook or expand the ward legal denominator to the
district-only Decree 09; the anomaly remains an explicit derived-topology
