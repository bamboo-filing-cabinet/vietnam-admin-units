# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 34/2006/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `34/2006/NĐ-CP@2006-04-15` now has two complete
commercial legal-database transcriptions, an indexed official VBPL record, and
official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization metadata. It remains
primary-source-open because no complete official page or original attachment
could be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The reviewed sources identify Decree 34/2006/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2006-03-31, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc thành lập thị trấn Cát Thành thuộc huyện Trực Ninh, tỉnh Nam Định

The three relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-03-31`;
- official effective date: `2006-04-25`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-04-15`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization gives 2006-04-25 as the
legal effective date. The NSO snapshot observes the conversion ten days
earlier. Its date remains an observation key and is not silently rewritten.

## Enacted operation and ward-level topology

Article 1 establishes Cát Thành township in Trực Ninh district from the entire
area and population of Cát Thành commune:

| Predecessor | Successor | Code | Area | Population |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Xã Cát Thành | Thị trấn Cát Thành | `14056` | 830.01 ha | 14,577 |

The new township borders Việt Hùng commune to the east, Trực Đạo commune to
the west, Hải Hậu district to the south, and Trực Tuấn commune to the north.
Because the complete predecessor territory and population pass to the
same-named successor, the decree creates no split and no new roster identity:
the administrative code survives while the Vietnamese unit label and type
change from `Xã` to `Thị trấn`.

The Cát Thành component of `soap:2006-04-07->2006-04-15` agrees exactly. Code
`14056` remains in Trực Ninh district and changes only `TenPhuongXa` and
`LoaiHinh`. Crosswalk row
`crosswalk/ward_2006-01-01_2007-01-01.xls#row=4830` independently assigns this
same-code component to Decree 34. The interval's other components are linked
to Decree 33 and are not attributed to this instrument.

date:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/HHTDanhMuc/Attachments/633/Tap%20HTH%202024.pdf>

These official indexed representations establish identity and dates, but they
do not satisfy the raw-artifact gate. Direct requests to the full-text, print,
Ministry of Home Affairs mirror, and properties endpoints returned HTTP 403
challenge pages. The indexed output did not expose an exact attachment
filename, so no filename is guessed or registered. The legacy
`vbpl.moj.gov.vn` hostname failed DNS resolution.

Fresh exact-code and title searches on the Government legal portal found no
matching Decree 34 record; the code search returned only unrelated substring
matches. A year-bounded Government Gazette API search returned no original
publication PDF.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with raw retrieval blocked. Primary
closure still requires saving the complete official page or an attachment it
actually exposes, recording its source metadata and hash, and verifying its
identity and contents offline.

## Commercial legal-database records

Thư Viện Pháp Luật publishes the complete enacted text:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-dinh-34-2006-ND-CP-thanh-lap-thi-tran-Cat-Thanh-thuoc-huyen-Truc-Ninh-tinh-Nam-Dinh-11014.aspx>

It includes the formal identity, recitals, whole-commune conversion, area,
population, four boundary descriptions, effectiveness clause, implementation
clause, and signature block. Classification: `secondary_full_text`.

LuatVietnam publishes a parallel complete transcription:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/chinh-sach/nghi-dinh-34-2006-nd-cp-chinh-phu-29367-d1.html>

It agrees on the formal identity, enacted operation, metrics, boundary
descriptions, three operative articles, and signatory. Its PDF and Word
downloads are member-gated commercial copies. Classification:
`secondary_full_text_parallel`.

No material disagreement was found between the two complete transcriptions.
Neither is an official enacted artifact, so they establish reviewed secondary
provenance without closing the primary-source gate.
