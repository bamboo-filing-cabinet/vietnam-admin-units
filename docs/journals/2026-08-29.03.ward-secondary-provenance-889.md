# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 889/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

`889/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12` remains primary-source-open, but three supplied
pages provide stronger secondary and official-secondary provenance for the
instrument's identity, issue date, and administrative scope.

## Official Government announcement

The Government News article published on 2015-03-28 records the ceremony
announcing the establishment of Điện Bàn town:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/cong-bo-thanh-lap-thi-xa-dien-ban-102181174.htm>

The article states that the Standing Committee of the National Assembly issued
instrument `889/NQ-UBTVQH13` on 2015-03-11, establishing Điện Bàn town with
21,471 hectares, 229,907 residents, and 20 commune-level units including seven
wards. Its photo caption also records that National Assembly Vice Chair Huỳnh
Ngọc Sơn presented the resolution at the ceremony.

The article calls the instrument a `Quyết định` in one sentence even though its
code and the rest of the evidence identify it as a `Nghị quyết`. That wording
is retained as an article-level error, not propagated into the legal registry.

Classification: `official_secondary`. This is a first-party Government report
of the resolution and its implementation, but it is not the complete enacted
text or an original publication file.

## LuatVietnam record and PDF preview

LuatVietnam identifies the same resolution, issuer, title, and issue date of
2015-03-11:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/dia-gioi-hanh-chinh/nghi-quyet-889-nq-ubtvqh13-2015-thanh-lap-thi-xa-dien-ban-tinh-quang-nam-272472-d1.html>

The page explicitly says its HTML content is still being updated and directs
readers to a PDF preview:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/van-ban/xem-truoc-file-edb063e3-0aa2-2b00-81eb-7ffea9f663e2>

Classification: `secondary_original_scan_lead`. The preview is useful evidence
that LuatVietnam holds a document image, but it is hosted by a commercial
secondary provider. It does not become an official original merely because it
reproduces one, and therefore does not close the primary-source gate.

TVPL also identifies Resolution 889 and its Điện Bàn/seven-ward scope:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-889-NQ-UBTVQH13-thanh-lap-thi-xa-07-phuong-thi-xa-Dien-Ban-Quang-Nam-2015-281497.aspx>

This URL was already present in the legal registry as a secondary identity
reference. The site returned a browser challenge during this review, so no new
raw bytes were accepted from it.

Classification: `secondary_identity_reference`.

## Registry treatment

The pinned NSO instrument key remains `889/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12`. The two
independent accessible pages above place issuance on 2015-03-11, one day before
the NSO date. That difference is useful provenance but does not establish the
resolution's formal effective date without the enacted text.

No source-status or coverage count changes result from this review. The TVPL
URL was already counted as secondary evidence, while the Government article
and LuatVietnam scan lead are preserved in this journal. Primary closure still
requires a complete enacted text or original PDF/DOC from an official National
Assembly, Government, VBPL, Gazette, or Quảng Nam government host.
