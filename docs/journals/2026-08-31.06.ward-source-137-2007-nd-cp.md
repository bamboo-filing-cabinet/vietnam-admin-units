# Ward official provenance: Decree 137/2007/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The supplied Government legal record closes primary provenance for the pinned
NSO instrument `137/2007N@2007-09-11`. The page exposes an original Word
attachment containing the complete enacted Decree 137/2007/NĐ-CP. Both files
are now archived, hashed, registered, and verified offline.

## Official identity and dates

The Government record identifies the instrument as Decree 137/2007/NĐ-CP,
issued on 2007-08-27, with the title:

> Về việc điều chỉnh địa giới hành chính xã để thành lập xã thuộc các huyện:
> Krông Ana, Ea Kar, Krông Búk, M'Drắk, Ea H'Leo; điều chỉnh địa giới hành
> chính huyện Krông Ana để thành lập huyện Cư Kuin, tỉnh Đắk Lắk.

The page is:

<https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?pageid=27160&docid=37761>

It exposes the original attachment directly:

<https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2007/09/39260_nd137ttg.doc>

The three retained date/code representations are intentionally distinct:

- formal code: `137/2007/NĐ-CP`;
- raw NSO index code: `137/2007N`;
- official issue date: `2007-08-27`;
- official legal effective date: `2007-09-18`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2007-09-11`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
Government record gives 2007-09-18 as the effective date. The earlier NSO date
is retained as the observation key rather than rewritten as legal
effectiveness. The raw title's `Krông An` is likewise retained beside the
official `Krông Ana` spelling.

## Cư Kuin creation and parent changes

Article 1.6 creates huyện Cư Kuin from 28,830 ha and 109,770 people of huyện
Krông Ana. It assigns eight communes to the new district:

Thus the interval contains seven continuing Đắk Lắk communes with parent
changes plus the newly created Ea Ning already attached to Cư Kuin. Crosswalk
rows 8466–8470 and 8473–8474 support the seven same-code moves and retain
Decree 137 in all or part of their legal-reference evidence.

The same SOAP interval also contains eleven Vĩnh Long parent moves, codes
`29773` through `29803`, from thị xã Bình Minh to newly created huyện Bình Tân.
Crosswalk rows 96–106 cite Decree 125/2007/NĐ-CP. Those concurrent changes are
not attributed to Decree 137 merely because the legal linkage operates at the
coarser observation-interval level.

## Source-text variants retained

The original Word attachment itself uses `Day Bhăng` in the first parenthetical
list in Article 1.6 and `Dray Bhăng` in the immediately following unit list. Its
final provincial summary also prints `M'Dắk`, while the title and preceding
operative clause use `M'Drắk`. The SOAP roster uses `Dray Bhăng` and
`M'Đrắk`. These are source-text variants, not grounds to alter the archived
bytes or silently normalize the legal transcription.

Thư Viện Pháp Luật publishes a complete parallel transcription and is retained
as secondary discovery/check evidence:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-dinh-137-2007-ND-CP-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-xa-de-thanh-lap-xa-thuoc-huyen-Krong-Ana-Ea-Kar-Krong-Buk-M-Drak-Ea-H-Leo-54828.aspx>

The supplied Government press report, published 2007-08-29, independently
confirms the decree number, five new commune names, Cư Kuin's eight constituent
communes, and its 28,830-ha/109,770-person basis. It reproduces the same
`Day Bhăng` and `M'Dắk` variants and is therefore corroboration, not the source
of record:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/thanh-lap-them-5-xa-va-1-huyen-thuoc-tinh-dak-lak-1024321.htm>
