# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 903/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `903/NQ-NBTVQH13@2015-04-11` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish complete secondary text
provenance and strong contemporaneous provincial corroboration, but none is a
complete official-hosted copy of the enacted resolution.

## Complete TVPL transcription

TVPL publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution 903/NQ-UBTVQH13:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-903-NQ-UBTVQH13-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-huyen-Ky-Anh-Ha-Tinh-2015-293976.aspx>

The transcription contains the formal header and 2015-04-10 issue date,
resolution title, recitals, all operative clauses, implementation clause,
effective-date clause, distribution list, and signature block for National
Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Article 1 transfers the former Kỳ Anh township and 11 communes from Kỳ Anh
district to establish Kỳ Anh town with 28,025.03 hectares, 85,508 residents,
and 12 commune-level units. It establishes six wards:

- Kỳ Liên from Kỳ Liên commune;
- Kỳ Long from Kỳ Long commune;
- Kỳ Phương from Kỳ Phương commune;
- Kỳ Thịnh from Kỳ Thịnh commune;
- Kỳ Trinh from Kỳ Trinh commune; and
- Sông Trí from Kỳ Anh township.

The post-adjustment Kỳ Anh town comprises those six wards and six communes.
Article 3 states that the resolution takes effect from the date of signing, so
the transcribed issue and effective date is 2015-04-10.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The transcription is complete enough
to support the resolution's identity, clauses, date, and signatory, but it is
commercially hosted rather than an official original artifact.

## Contemporaneous announcement

Báo Hà Tĩnh reported the formal announcement ceremony on 2015-05-16:

<https://baohatinh.vn/cong-bo-nghi-quyet-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-de-thanh-lap-thi-xa-ky-anh-post96025.html>

The report identifies Resolution 903/NQ-UBTVQH13, its 2015-04-10 date, Kỳ Anh
town and six-ward operation, 28,025.03-hectare area, 85,508 population, and the
11 communes plus former township used to form the town. It also records that
the resolution was announced by a National Assembly Office official at a
ceremony attended by National Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Classification: `state_press_secondary`. This is strong contemporaneous
corroboration, not the enacted artifact itself. The 2015-05-16 announcement
ceremony is not the legal effective date.

## Registry treatment

The complete transcription and every source that prints the full code confirm
`903/NQ-UBTVQH13`. The pinned NSO code `903/NQ-NBTVQH13` therefore contains a
transposition error. The raw-derived instrument key is retained for stable
linkage, while the formal code is recorded here.

The pinned NSO date remains 2015-04-11. The complete transcription and
independent provincial reports instead place issuance on 2015-04-10, and the
take-effect-on-signing clause makes that the transcribed effective date as
well. The one-day discrepancy is preserved rather than normalized away.

No source-status or coverage count changes result from this review. Primary
closure still requires a complete enacted text or original PDF/DOC from an
official National Assembly, Government, VBPL, Gazette, or Hà Tĩnh government
host.
