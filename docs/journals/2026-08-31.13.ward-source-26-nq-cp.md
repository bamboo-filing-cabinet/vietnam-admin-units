# Ward official provenance: Resolution 26/NQ-CP (Thuận Nam)

Date: 2026-08-31

record for `26/NQ-CP@2009-06-11`. The page and its original Word attachment are
now archived, hashed, registered, and verified offline. This closes the
instrument's primary-source requirement without promoting any secondary page

<https://www.qdnd.vn/chinh-tri/tin-tuc/ngay-9-5-2009-cong-bo-thanh-lap-thanh-pho-hung-yen-278962>

<https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?pageid=27160&docid=88122>

The report is therefore excluded from the Decree 14 row. No primary bytes for

## Official identity and dates

The exact Government record is:

<https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?pageid=27160&docid=88007>

It exposes the original Word attachment directly:

<https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2009/06/91250_nq26cp.doc>

The parallel official VBPL full-text record is Item 11902:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=11902>

The Government page and Word file identify Resolution 26/NQ-CP, signed by
Prime Minister Nguyễn Tấn Dũng on `2009-06-10`. Article 2 makes it effective
on the date of signature. The pinned NSO instrument date `2009-06-11` is the
first observed roster date and remains an observation key; it is not rewritten
as the legal effective date.

## Enacted commune and district changes

Article 1 first creates two communes in Ninh Phước:

| New commune | NSO code | Source | Area | Population |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Phước Ninh | `22898` | Phước Nam | 2,686.69 ha | 4,292 |
| Cà Ná | `22910` | Phước Diêm | 1,307.82 ha | 8,537 |

It then creates huyện Thuận Nam from 56,452.62 ha and 54,768 people of
Ninh Phước. The new district contains eight communes: Cà Ná, Phước Diêm,
Phước Ninh, Phước Nam, Phước Minh, Phước Dinh, Nhị Hà, and Phước
Hà. Residual Ninh Phước has 34,103.37 ha, 135,146 people, and nine
commune-level units.

## Supplied-source assessment

The six supplied pages are retained or bounded as follows:

- Nhân Dân confirms Cà Ná's creation under Resolution 26 and provides later
  new-rural-development context. Its contemporary area and population figures
  are not substituted for the enacted 2009 figures:
  <https://nhandan.vn/ninh-thuan-xa-ca-na-dat-chuan-nong-thon-moi-nang-cao-post761802.html>
- Báo Khánh Hòa's 2012 administrative history accurately summarizes the two
  commune creations and Thuận Nam's eight-unit roster. It is historical
  corroboration, not an original legal artifact:
  <https://baokhanhhoa.vn/quehuong/23039p142c153/don-vi-hanh-chinh-tinh-ninh-thuan-sau-20-nam-tai-lap-1992-2012.htm/>
- Thư Viện Pháp Luật publishes a complete transcription and is registered as
  the deterministic secondary identity reference:
  <https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-26-NQ-CP-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-xa-de-lap-xa-thuoc-huyen-Ninh-Phuoc-huyen-Ninh-Phuo71c-de-thanh-lap-huyen-Thuan-Nam-Ninh-Thuan-89680.aspx>
- LuatVietnam provides another transcription and a paywalled scan lead, but
  its visible text drops `Nam` from both `huyện Thuận Nam` in the title and
  `xã Phước Nam` in the Phước Ninh clause. The official Word file controls:
  <https://luatvietnam.vn/dat-dai/nghi-quyet-26-nq-cp-chinh-phu-42854-d1.html>
- The Vietnam News Agency follow-up says the district administration began
  operating on `2009-10-01`. That operational date is useful implementation

  <https://happyvietnam.vnanet.vn/khat-vong-dua-vung-dat-kho-han-thuan-nam-tro-thanh-vung-kinh-te-trong-diem/38863.html>

  Ninh Phước population `573,925` is a transcription error: that is the
  province-wide population in the resolution. The enacted residual-district
  figure is `135,146`:
  <https://baochinhphu.vn/thanh-lap-moi-huyen-thuan-nam-thuoc-tinh-ninh-thuan-10224590.htm>
