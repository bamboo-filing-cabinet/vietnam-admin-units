## Enacted changes

Article 1 expands thành phố Mỹ Tho by 3,295.28 hectares and 31,357 people from
Châu Thành and Chợ Gạo districts. The transferred territory comprises all of
xã Thới Sơn and portions of Long An, Thạnh Phú, Phước Thạnh, Bình Đức, Lương
Hòa Lạc, and Song Bình.

The resolution then:

- establishes xã Phước Thạnh in Mỹ Tho from 1,017.60 hectares and 12,105
  people transferred from Long An, Thạnh Phú, and the former Phước Thạnh;
- transfers specified residual areas and populations into Trung An, Đạo
  Thạnh, and Tân Mỹ Chánh; and
- transfers the former Phước Thạnh's remaining 323.14 hectares and 3,093
  people into Thạnh Phú.

The official PDF controls where parallel transcriptions differ.
