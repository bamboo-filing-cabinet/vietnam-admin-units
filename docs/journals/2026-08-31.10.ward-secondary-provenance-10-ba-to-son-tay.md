## The decree is from 2008

The formal decree was issued on `2008-12-23`. The repository suffix
`@2009-01-07` is the pinned NSO registry/observation date, not the decree's
issue year or its legal effective date.

The official VBPL record is Item 12653:

- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12653>
- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=12653&Keyword=>

Its indexed properties and rendered text identify Decree 10/NĐ-CP, signed by
Prime Minister Nguyễn Tấn Dũng, concerning commune-boundary adjustments in Ba
Tơ and Sơn Tây districts, Quảng Ngãi. The relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2008-12-23`;
- Gazette publication date: `2009-01-03`, issues 05 and 06;
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2009-01-07`; and
- official effective date: `2009-01-18`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
NSO date is therefore retained as an observation key rather than silently
rewritten as legal effectiveness. The official record advertises original
attachment `10.NĐ.CP.zip`; the filename is registered as a recovery lead, but
no unobserved attachment URL is guessed.

## Enacted commune creations

The complete TVPL transcription is:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-dinh-10-ND-CP-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-xa-thanh-lap-thuoc-huyen-ba-to-huyen-Son-Tay-tinh-Quang-Ngai-84048.aspx>

Article 1 creates four communes:

| New commune | NSO code | District | Source commune | Territory and population |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Ba Giang | `21500` | Ba Tơ | Ba Dinh | 5,419.82 ha; 1,521 people |
| Sơn Long | `21341` | Sơn Tây | Sơn Dung | 4,240.67 ha; 2,154 people |
| Sơn Liên | `21335` | Sơn Tây | Sơn Mùa | 3,718.23 ha; 1,535 people |
| Sơn Màu | `21338` | Sơn Tây | Sơn Tân | 3,718.23 ha; 1,599 people |

The post-adjustment text leaves Ba Dinh with 3,575.95 hectares and 3,685
people, and leaves Sơn Dung, Sơn Mùa, and Sơn Tân with their continuing
identities. Ba Tơ then has 20 commune-level units; Sơn Tây has nine.

Classification: `official_central_contemporaneous_summary`. It is strong
official corroboration for the decree's number, territory, timing, and four
new commune names, but it does not close primary provenance.

Đà Lạt city and Đức Trọng and Di Linh districts, Lâm Đồng. It is already
represented by `10/NĐ-CP@2009-03-21`, with archived Government metadata and
its original Word attachment.

The Ba Tơ–Sơn Tây TVPL URL is therefore assigned through a date-qualified key
only to `10/NĐ-CP@2009-01-07`. An explicit empty override protects the later
Lâm Đồng row from same-code contamination.
