# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 932/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `932/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-05-16` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish complete secondary text
provenance, official National Assembly press corroboration for the Phổ Yên
portion, and downstream implementation evidence, but none is a complete
official-hosted copy of the enacted resolution.

## Complete TVPL transcription

TVPL publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution 932/NQ-UBTVQH13:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-932-NQ-UBTVQH13-thanh-lap-thi-xa-Pho-Yen-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-Thai-Nguyen-289498.aspx>

The transcription contains the formal header and 2015-05-15 issue date,
resolution title, recitals, every operative clause, implementation clause,
effective-date clause, distribution list, and signature block for National
Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Article 1 contains three connected administrative changes:

1. It establishes Phổ Yên town from the full 25,886.9 hectares and 158,619
   residents of Phổ Yên district, together with four wards:
   - Ba Hàng: 444.53 hectares and 10,139 residents, formed from Ba Hàng
     township and part of Đồng Tiến commune;
   - Bãi Bông: 350.65 hectares and 5,614 residents, formed from Bãi Bông
     township;
   - Bắc Sơn: 369.09 hectares and 5,231 residents, formed from Bắc Sơn
     township; and
   - Đồng Tiến: the remaining 780.92 hectares and 16,314 residents of Đồng
     Tiến commune.
2. It transfers all 1,560.8 hectares and 23,865 residents of Lương Sơn commune
   from Thái Nguyên city to Sông Công town and establishes Lương Sơn ward from
   that territory.
3. It establishes Sông Công city from the full 9,837.07 hectares and 109,409
   residents of Sông Công town.

The resulting Phổ Yên town has 18 commune-level units: four wards and 14
communes. Sông Công city has 11: seven wards and four communes. Article 3 says
that the resolution takes effect from the date of signing, making the
transcribed issue and effective date 2015-05-15.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The page supports the complete legal
identity, clauses, figures, issue/effective date, and signatory, but its
commercial host is not the official enacted artifact.

## National Assembly press corroboration

Đại Biểu Nhân Dân's 2022 retrospective on Phổ Yên's development records the
2015 legal transition:

<https://daibieunhandan.vn/chang-duong-but-toc-10294687.html>

It identifies Resolution 932/NQ-UBTVQH13, the National Assembly Standing
Committee, and the 2015-05-15 date. It independently repeats the creation of
Phổ Yên town from 25,886.9 hectares and 158,619 residents and names the four
wards Ba Hàng, Bãi Bông, Đồng Tiến, and Bắc Sơn.

Classification: `official_national_assembly_press_secondary`. This is strong
official-hosted corroboration for the Phổ Yên portion, but it is a later
editorial article rather than the enacted text and does not cover the Lương Sơn
transfer or Sông Công city clauses.

## Downstream road-naming decision

Thư Viện Nhà Đất republishes Thái Nguyên Provincial People's Committee
Decision 15/2015/QĐ-UBND dated 2015-06-18:

<https://thuviennhadat.vn/van-ban-phap-luat-viet-nam/quyet-dinh-15-2015-qd-ubnd-dat-ten-19-tuyen-duong-phuong-ba-hang-thi-xa-pho-yen-tinh-thai-nguyen-289783.html>

The decision names 19 roads in Ba Hàng ward, Phổ Yên town, showing provincial
administrative use of the new place and unit types about one month after
Resolution 932. Its reproduced legal bases do not cite Resolution 932, and it
does not describe the target resolution's formation clauses.

Classification: `secondary_downstream_implementation`. It is circumstantial
implementation evidence, not direct provenance for Resolution 932.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO instrument key uses `2015-05-16`. The complete TVPL
transcription and the independent National Assembly press article both place
issuance on `2015-05-15`; the transcribed Article 3 makes that signing date the
effective date as well. The one-day discrepancy is preserved rather than
silently normalized.
