# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 934/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `934/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-05-16` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish complete secondary text
provenance and strong contemporary Government and press corroboration, but
none is a complete official-hosted copy of the enacted resolution.

## Complete TVPL transcription

TVPL publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution 934/NQ-UBTVQH13:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-934-NQ-UBTVQH13-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-huyen-Tra-Cu-Duyen-Hai-Tra-Vinh-2015-293985.aspx>

The transcription contains the formal header and 2015-05-15 issue date,
resolution title, recitals, all operative and resulting-topology clauses,
implementation clause, effective-date clause, distribution list, and
signature block for National Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Article 1 records the connected changes:

1. It transfers all 3,128.81 hectares and 12,667 residents of Đôn Châu commune
   and all 2,621 hectares and 14,808 residents of Đôn Xuân commune from Trà Cú
   district to Duyên Hải district.
2. It transfers 1,531.40 hectares and 2,711 residents from Dân Thành commune to
   Đông Hải commune in Duyên Hải district.
3. It establishes Duyên Hải town from 17,709.64 hectares and 56,241 residents
   comprising Duyên Hải township and the communes Hiệp Thạnh, Long Hữu, Long
   Toàn, Trường Long Hòa, and the post-adjustment Dân Thành.
4. It establishes Phường 1 from all 200.54 hectares and 12,493 residents of
   Duyên Hải township plus 1,150 hectares and 1,747 residents of Long Toàn
   commune, producing a final ward of 1,350.54 hectares and 14,240 residents.
5. It establishes Phường 2 from 790.67 hectares and 6,253 residents of Long
   Toàn commune plus 362.73 hectares and 6,456 residents of Long Hữu commune,
   producing a final ward of 1,153.40 hectares and 12,709 residents.

The resulting Duyên Hải town has seven commune-level units: Phường 1, Phường
2, and the communes Dân Thành, Hiệp Thạnh, Long Hữu, Long Toàn, and Trường
Long Hòa. The successor Duyên Hải district has Long Thành township and six
communes: Đông Hải, Đôn Châu, Đôn Xuân, Long Khánh, Long Vĩnh, and Ngũ Lạc.
Article 3 says that the resolution takes effect on its signing date, making
the transcribed issue and effective date 2015-05-15.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The commercial page supports the
complete legal identity, clauses, figures, resulting topology, issue/effective
date, and signatory, but it is not the official enacted artifact.

## Government press corroboration

Báo Điện tử Chính phủ's contemporary report covers the provincial ceremony
announcing Resolution 934:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/thanh-lap-thi-xa-duyen-hai-tinh-tra-vinh-102188716.htm>

It identifies the exact code and title, Nguyễn Sinh Hùng as signatory, and the
2015-05-15 signing date. It independently repeats the resulting Duyên Hải town
area, population, two wards, five communes, and boundaries; the successor
Duyên Hải district's area, population, township, and six communes; Trà Cú's
post-adjustment figures and units; and the province-wide totals. It also
records that a National Assembly Standing Committee member formally presented
the resolution to the new town's leadership.

The article credits `Travinh.gov.vn`, so it is best understood as an
official-hosted republication of provincial reporting rather than a second
copy of the enacted instrument.

Classification: `official_government_press_secondary`. This is strong
official-hosted contemporary corroboration, but it does not reproduce every
formation clause or the resolution's implementation and effective-date text.

## State-press summary and ward-figure caveat

Báo Nông nghiệp và Môi trường's report covers the 2015-08-15 announcement
ceremony:

<https://nongnghiepmoitruong.vn/thanh-lap-thi-xa-duyen-hai-d147984.html>

It identifies Resolution 934/NQ-UBTVQH13, the 2015-05-15 date, the Trà Cú and
Duyên Hải adjustment, creation of Duyên Hải town and two wards, approximate
town and successor-district areas, exact populations, and the resulting
province-wide unit counts.

Its ward figures require caution. The article describes Phường 1 as more than
200 hectares with 12,493 residents and Phường 2 as more than 790 hectares with
6,253 residents. Those are only the Duyên Hải township and Long Toàn component
figures, respectively. The complete transcription shows that each ward also
received additional territory and population, producing final totals of
1,350.54 hectares and 14,240 residents for Phường 1 and 1,153.40 hectares and
12,709 residents for Phường 2.

Classification: `state_press_secondary_summary`. It corroborates the event
identity and implementation ceremony, but its abbreviated ward figures must
not be used as final post-resolution totals.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO instrument key uses `2015-05-16`, the first relevant observed
snapshot date. The complete TVPL transcription and all three contemporary
reports identify Resolution 934 as signed on `2015-05-15`; the transcribed
Article 3 makes that signing date the effective date. The one-day discrepancy
is preserved rather than silently changing the pinned observation-based key.
