## Identity, code correction, and dates

The raw NSO legal index uses `28/2006/NDD-CP`. Both the Government metadata and
the original attachment use the legally correct code `28/2006/NĐ-CP`. The
registry preserves the raw code as the instrument key and records the official
code separately with `code_match_status: official_code_differs`; no raw index
row is rewritten.

The relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-03-22`;
- official effective date: `2006-04-13`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-04-06`.

Article 2 says the decree takes effect 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL record gives 2006-04-13 as the legal effective date. The NSO
snapshot observes the new unit seven days earlier, so the registry retains its
date as an observation key and records `effective_date_match_status:
index_effective_date_differs`.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 first transfers population into Sa Nhơn commune:

- 324 people from Sa Nghĩa commune, Sa Thầy district;
- 2,635 people from Hà Mòn commune, Đắk Hà district; and
- 573 people from Kroong commune, Kon Tum town.

It then creates Hơ Moong commune from 6,523 ha and 3,532 people of Sa Nhơn.
Hơ Moong consequently has 6,523 ha and 3,532 people; Sa Nhơn retains 5,177 ha
and 2,461 people. Sa Thầy district has 11 subordinate units after the change:
Mô Rai, Rờ Kơi, Sa Nhơn, Sa Sơn, Sa Nghĩa, Sa Bình, Ya Xier, Ya Ly, Ya Tăng,
Hơ Moong, and Sa Thầy township.

The `soap:2006-04-05->2006-04-06` observation contains exactly one addition:
Hơ Moong commune (`23534`) under Sa Thầy district, Kon Tum province. It has no
removals or same-code identity changes. The population transfers and Sa Nhơn
boundary reduction therefore remain legal context rather than invented roster
changes.
