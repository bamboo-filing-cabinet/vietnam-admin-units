# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 07/NĐ-CP (Buôn Hồ)

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `07/NĐ-CP@2009-01-07` now has a complete commercial
legal-database transcription and an exact official VBPL record that advertises
the original ZIP. It remains primary-source-open because the official page and
ZIP could not be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Same-code source disambiguation

The two supplied URLs do not describe the same enactment.

The TVPL page is the Buôn Hồ instrument under review:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-dinh-07-ND-CP-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-xa-thuoc-huyen-Krong-Buk-thanh-lap-thi-xa-Buon-Ho-phuong-thuoc-tinh-Dak-Lak-84045.aspx>

The LuatVietnam page is a later Decree 07/NĐ-CP, issued on 2009-02-09, about
commune changes in Tương Dương, Thanh Chương, Anh Sơn, Yên Thành, and Hưng
Nguyên districts of Nghệ An:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/chinh-sach/nghi-dinh-07-nd-cp-chinh-phu-40226-d1.html>

That later instrument is already represented by
`07/NĐ-CP@2009-02-24`, with archived Government metadata and its original Word
attachment. It has no observable SOAP component in its interval. The
LuatVietnam page is therefore excluded from the Buôn Hồ row rather than
treated as corroboration.

This exposed a deterministic provenance bug: the older secondary URL mapping
was keyed only by instrument code, so the Buôn Hồ TVPL URL was also attached
to the later Nghệ An record. `data/ward-legal-secondary-urls.json` now supports
date-qualified keys and explicit empty overrides. The Buôn Hồ URL is assigned
only to `07/NĐ-CP@2009-01-07`; the incorrect reference was removed from the
Nghệ An registry and both of its raw-manifest entries.

## Official identity and dates

The official VBPL record is Item 12658:

- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12658>
- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=12658&Keyword=>

Its indexed properties identify Decree 07/NĐ-CP, signed by Prime Minister
Nguyễn Tấn Dũng, with the Buôn Hồ administrative-boundary title. The four
relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2008-12-23`;
- Gazette publication date: `2009-01-03`, issues 05 and 06;
- official effective date: `2009-01-18`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2009-01-07`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
NSO date therefore remains an observation key and is not silently rewritten
as the legal effective date.

The official page advertises original attachment `07.NĐ.CP.zip`. That exact
filename is recorded as a recovery lead; no attachment URL is guessed.

## Enacted operations and observed topology

The complete TVPL transcription records boundary adjustments among Thống
Nhất, Ea Blang, Bình Thuận, Cư Bao, and Ea Đê before creating thị xã Buôn Hồ
from 28,205.89 hectares and 101,554 people of huyện Krông Búk. It establishes
seven wards:

- Đạt Hiếu;
- An Lạc;
- An Bình;
- Thiện An;
- Đoàn Kết;
- Thống Nhất; and
- Bình Tân.

Buôn Hồ also retains the five communes Ea Siên, Ea Drông, Ea Blang, Bình
Thuận, and Cư Bao. The remainder of Ea Đê is renamed Tân Lập, while huyện
Krông Búk continues with seven communes.

The SOAP interval `soap:2009-01-01->2009-01-07` contains five candidate legal
instruments and 83 components. Crosswalk citations and named-unit matching
assign 23 components to this decree: 15 `lineage`, five
`parent_or_boundary_only`, and three `rename_or_retype`. This topology is
consistent with the secondary text, but it does not substitute for an
archived official artifact.

The TVPL transcription appears to render Phường Đạt Hiếu with only
`109 nhân khẩu`, even though its source parcel is stated as 7,109 people and
the resulting totals require 7,109. The secondary text is not silently
corrected. The original official ZIP must adjudicate whether this is a TVPL
transcription error.

## Official-source recovery boundary

Direct requests to the official full-text and properties pages returned HTTP
403 browser challenges. The in-app browser workflow was also unavailable, so
the advertised ZIP could not be saved interactively.

Bounded exact-code and Buôn Hồ title searches on the Government legal portal
found no copy of this enactment; the only Decree 07 result was the later Nghệ
An instrument. Government Gazette API searches returned no match, and the
live Gazette web archive stops at July 2010 rather than reaching January 2009.
Official national and local search results corroborated the record's identity
or Buôn Hồ's creation but did not expose the complete enacted text or original
bytes.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with original attachment indexed
and raw retrieval blocked. Primary closure still requires saving the complete
official page and `07.NĐ.CP.zip`, recording their provenance and hashes, and
verifying the identity, dates, operations, and Đạt Hiếu population offline.
