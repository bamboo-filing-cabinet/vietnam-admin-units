# Day-level change dates are available — and a snapshot-drift caveat

Written 2026-07-10. Answers "can we get exact day-level change dates, or do we
need more granular capture?" — and documents a reliability wrinkle found while
checking.

## Yes: day-level precision is available (two ways)

1. **SOAP `DenNgay` is day-precise and bisectable.** Bisecting the national ward
   count pinned the 2019-wave drop to exactly **2020-01-01** (11,075→10,797).
   Earlier bisects nailed 2008-08-01, 2025-07-01, 2004-06-30, 2002-01-01,
   2026-04-30. The service reconstructs the hierarchy for any calendar day.
2. **Lịch Sử change-log gives the effective date + decree directly** (per unit),
   so the day-level date is stated, not inferred.

A single year has **multiple distinct change days** — monthly ward counts across
2019 stepped down in ~Mar, ~Jun, ~Nov, Dec, and 2020-01-01 (≈5 dates). Admin
changes are day-level by decree; **no sub-day granularity is needed or exists.**

## Preferred source for dating: Lịch Sử, not SOAP bisection

- Lịch Sử states the effective date + Nghị quyết → no need to bisect thousands
  of ward transitions (a wave-year has many distinct dates).
- SOAP historical snapshots are **not guaranteed stable over time** (below), so
  bisected dates could move; the decree-linked Lịch Sử dates are authoritative.

This reinforces the hybrid change-discovery decision (`.11`, DESIGN): SOAP for
membership snapshots, **Lịch Sử for the authoritative event dates + decrees**.

## The drift caveat (reliability finding)

Observed within a single session (~15 min apart), same `DenNgay`:

| date | `.13` run | later run | Δ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2019-01-01 | 11,164 | 11,181 | +17 |
| 2020-01-01 | 10,778 | 10,797 | +19 |
| 30/06/2025 | 10,035 | 10,035 | 0 |

- **Not per-call randomness:** 4 immediate repeats of each date were identical.
  The value *shifted between runs* and then held.
- **Not a diffgram `before`-block artifact:** the responses had no `<diffgr:before>`,
  and DocumentElement-scoped counts equalled raw counts.
- So the service's **reconstruction of a past date changed by ~18 records** —
  most likely GSO backfilling/correcting historical data, or cache-node
  inconsistency. The current-era date (30/06/2025) was stable.

**Consequences:**

1. **Pin the raw, don't trust upstream immutability.** The verbatim-raw +
   `manifest.jsonl` (sha256 + retrieved_at) decision (`.06`/DESIGN) is load-
   bearing: our dataset is fixed to exactly what we fetched. Re-fetches may
   differ; the manifest records provenance + hash so drift is detectable.
2. **Treat exact historical counts as soft (±~20).** The churn figures in `.12`
   and `.13` were a snapshot of a drifting source; the *patterns* (waves in
   2019–21 and 2023–25, ~5 change-dates/year, province count stable at 63) are
   robust, the precise integers are not.
3. **Date lineage from decrees, not diffs.** Use Lịch Sử effective dates for
   `P571`/`P576`/`P585`; use SOAP diffs only to *detect* that a change occurred
   and cross-check membership.
