## Enacted changes

Article 1 establishes Ninh Cường township from the entire 7.41 km² and 10,244
residents of Trực Phú commune, Trực Ninh district. Its stated boundaries are:

- east: Trực Cường commune, Trực Ninh district;
- west: Trực Hùng commune, Trực Ninh district;
- south: Hải Toàn and Hải An communes, Hải Hậu district; and
- north: Trực Thuận and Trực Mỹ communes, Trực Ninh district, and Liễu Đề
  township, Nghĩa Hưng district.

After the conversion, Trực Ninh district has 21 commune-level units: 18
communes and three townships. Nam Định province has ten district-level units,
comprising nine districts and one city, and 229 commune-level units,
comprising 193 communes, 20 wards, and 16 townships.

Article 2 directs the Government, the provincial People's Council and People's
Committee, and related bodies to implement the resolution, stabilize local
institutions and residents' lives, and support socioeconomic development,
defense, and security. Article 3 makes the resolution effective on its signing
date.

## Supplied-source assessment

### National Assembly Vietlaw

The later-supplied Vietlaw URL is the controlling provenance. It supplies both
the complete official rendered text and an official downloadable Word file.
Unlike a news report or commercial legal transcription, it closes the enacted-
text requirement directly on a National Assembly host.

The live site initially returns a 177-byte JavaScript response that sets a
strict 32-character `D1N` reload cookie. The existing official-source fetcher
already handles that bounded challenge. The National Assembly parser now also
supports Vietlaw's page structure: breadcrumb identity in place of an `h1`, an
empty description meta tag, formal issue-date text, explicit effective-date
metadata, title paragraphs in the enacted-text container, and the direct
download link.
