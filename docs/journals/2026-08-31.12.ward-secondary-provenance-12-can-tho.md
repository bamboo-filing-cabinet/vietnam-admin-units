## The decree is from 2008

The official record is VBPL Item 12650:

- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12650>
- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=12650&Keyword=>

The record identifies Decree 12/NĐ-CP, signed by Prime Minister Nguyễn Tấn
Dũng, concerning commune, ward, and district changes in Cần Thơ. The relevant
dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2008-12-23`;
- Gazette publication date: `2009-01-03`, issues 05 and 06;
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2009-01-07`; and
- official effective date: `2009-01-18`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
NSO date is retained as an observation key rather than silently rewritten as
legal effectiveness. The official record advertises original attachment
`12.NĐ.CP.zip`; no attachment URL is inferred from that filename.

The decree also renames the residual xã Thới Lai to xã Thới Tân. Part III
creates huyện Thới Lai from Cờ Đỏ and transfers the named continuing units.
Other clauses transfer Vĩnh Bình to Vĩnh Thạnh and Trung An, Trung Thạnh,
Thạnh Phú, and Trung Hưng to Cờ Đỏ, with partial boundary transfers among
several source communes.

The official text first describes Thới Lai as 13 commune-level units and
enumerates 12 xã plus thị trấn Thới Lai. Its later summary says `12` units but
repeats the same 13-name roster. The enumeration and observed roster control;
the later number is preserved as an internal text inconsistency rather than
used to remove a unit.

## Supplied-source assessment

The five supplied URLs were reviewed as follows:

- TVPL is the complete parallel legal transcription already used as the
  deterministic identity reference.
- The THVL URL is a provincial-broadcaster announcement link, but the page did
  not render in the available retrieval path. A syndicated copy of the same
  title describes the 27 February 2009 announcement; the unavailable original
  is not promoted to evidence.
- Dân trí's `2009-02-26` report contemporaneously records the proclamation of
  Decree 12, quận Thốt Nốt, its nine wards, and huyện Thới Lai. Its prose says
  `13 xã` plus a town while enumerating only 12 xã plus the town, so the
  complete decree controls the count.
- Sài Gòn Giải Phóng's `2009-05-27` report confirms the 1 March proclamation
  and the Cờ Đỏ–Thới Lai district split while discussing later planning
  effects. It is implementation context, not enacted text.
- Báo Cần Thơ's follow-up identifies the `2008-12-23` decree and discusses the
  new Cờ Đỏ administration after the Thới Lai split. It is useful local
  implementation corroboration, not a primary copy.

None of the four press pages closes primary provenance.
