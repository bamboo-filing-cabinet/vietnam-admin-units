# Province lineage & Ghi Chú parseability (the Phase-1 validation set)

Written 2026-07-10. Corrects an earlier framing (that province-first "sidesteps"
`Ghi Chú` parsing) and grounds the Phase-1 plan. Based on the province-level
crosswalk export (`… cấp Tỉnh _ 30_06_2025 và 10_07_2026.xls`, 63 rows).

## Provinces have the SAME lineage structure as wards

Base = pre-reform (63 provinces) → comparison = current:

- **34 rows carry a structured `Tỉnh ĐC`** (successor code) — the "primary /
  survivor" of each new province. Includes the code-reassignment case:
  **`10` Lào Cai → `15` Lào Cai** (survivor keeps the *name*, takes Yên Bái's
  old *code* 15).
- **29 rows have blank `Tỉnh ĐC`** — the **absorbed** provinces. Their
  destination is **not** in the columns; it is **only in `Ghi Chú`**.

So the structured column alone is incomplete for merges — exactly as at ward
level (`2026-07-10.06`). Complete lineage needs `Ghi Chú`. Province-first does
**not** avoid parsing; it is the place to *build and validate* it.

## The Ghi Chú is a regular, parseable template

Merged provinces carry a near-fixed template:

> *"Sắp xếp toàn bộ diện tích tự nhiên, quy mô dân số của **{A}**, **{B}** (và
> **{C}**) thành {tỉnh|thành phố} mới có tên gọi là **{W}**"*

Unchanged provinces: *"Giữ nguyên, không sắp xếp."* A one-line regex —
`của (.+?) thành (?:tỉnh|thành phố) mới có tên gọi là (.+)`, splitting the first
group on `, ` / ` và ` — extracted **12 merge edges cleanly**, all matching
known reality:

- Hà Giang + Tuyên Quang → Tuyên Quang
- Bắc Kạn + Thái Nguyên → Thái Nguyên
- **Yên Bái + Lào Cai → Lào Cai**
- Vĩnh Phúc + Hòa Bình + Phú Thọ → Phú Thọ
- Bắc Giang + Bắc Ninh → Bắc Ninh
- …

Remaining rows are a small number of template *variants* — city establishments
("Thành lập thành phố Huế … trên cơ sở …") and the centrally-run-city merges
("… thành **thành phố** mới …" for Hải Phòng/Đà Nẵng/HCMC/Cần Thơ). A handful of
patterns covers all 63. The note appears on the **absorbed** constituent(s); the
surviving-name province's row is blank (its identity continues).

## Why provinces are the ideal parser testbed

The 63→34 reform is fully documented, so every parsed edge has known ground
truth. We can assert the parser reproduces exactly the 34 results (11 unchanged +
23 merged-away-into) before pointing the same parser at 10,040 ward `Ghi Chú`
rows, where the templates are the same verbs (`hợp nhất`, `đổi tên`, `một phần`,
`sáp nhập`) but no full ground-truth list exists.

## Consequences for the design/plan

- **`Ghi Chú` parsing is a Phase-1 (province) deliverable**, not deferred. Build
  a rule-based template parser; validate to 100% against the 34 known outcomes.
- Lineage inputs combine cleanly: structured `Tỉnh ĐC` = primary/survivor edge
  (incl. code change); parsed `Ghi Chú` = the absorbed→survivor edges + full
  constituent set; SOAP snapshot diff (63 vs 34) = cross-check that every
  province is accounted for.
- The parser is the reusable core carried into the ward phase.
