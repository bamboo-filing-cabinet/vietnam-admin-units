# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 933/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

This is a follow-up to the number-collision review in
`2026-08-29.10.ward-source-number-collision-933.md`. The newly supplied pages
concern the correct legislature-XIII instrument and establish strong secondary
and official retrospective provenance for the Long Mỹ event.

The pinned NSO instrument `933/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-05-16` nevertheless remains
primary-source-open. None of the supplied pages is the complete enacted
resolution on an official host.

## Statistical implementation table

Luật Việt Nam publishes a complete transcription of General Statistics Office
Letter 546/TCTK-PPCĐ dated 2015-07-30, signed by Director General Nguyễn Bích
Lâm:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/hanh-chinh/cong-van-546-tctk-ppcd-tong-cuc-thong-ke-96986-d6.html>

The letter instructs ministries, Government agencies, and provincial
governments to use the attached new administrative-unit codes. Its Hậu Giang
table explicitly associates `933/NQ-UBTVQH13` with 2015-05-15 and records the
resulting Long Mỹ topology:

- district code 936: Long Mỹ town, changed from Long Mỹ district;
- commune code 31471: Thuận An ward, changed from Long Mỹ township;
- commune code 31472: Trà Lồng ward, changed from Trà Lồng township;
- commune code 31473: Bình Thạnh ward, split from Long Mỹ township and Long
  Bình commune;
- commune code 31475: Vĩnh Tường ward, split from Long Bình commune; and
- district code 937: the successor Long Mỹ district, split from the former
  Long Mỹ district, containing Thuận Hưng, Thuận Hòa, Vĩnh Thuận Đông, Vĩnh
  Viễn, Vĩnh Viễn A, Lương Tâm, Lương Nghĩa, and Xà Phiên communes.

Classification: `secondary_official_document_transcription`. The underlying
letter is an official downstream implementation instrument and strongly
verifies the target resolution's code, date, place, town and ward creations,
successor district, and statistical codes. The accessible copy is on a
commercial legal host, however, and the letter does not reproduce Resolution
933's recitals, area/population figures, implementation clauses, signature, or
effective-date clause.

## Official National Assembly retrospective

The National Assembly portal's print page publishes a 2019-03-06 report on the
Law Committee's review of the proposed Vĩnh Viễn township:

<https://quochoi.vn/UserControls/Publishing/News/BinhLuan/pFormPrint.aspx?UrlListProcess=/content/tintuc/Lists/News&ItemID=39383>

The report attributes the underlying statement to Deputy Minister of Home
Affairs Trần Anh Tuấn. It says that implementing Resolution
933/NQ-UBTVQH13 dated 2015-05-15 established Long Mỹ town and left the
successor Long Mỹ district with 260.07 square kilometres, 84,423 residents,
and eight communes, but no district-seat township.

Đại Biểu Nhân Dân's 2019-03-12 report independently repeats the same legal
identity and consequences while covering the Standing Committee's later
consideration of Vĩnh Viễn township:

<https://daibieunhandan.vn/xem-xet-quyet-dinh-viec-thanh-lap-thi-xa-my-hao-hung-yen-va-thi-tran-vinh-vien-huyen-long-my-hau-giang-10227383.html>

Classification: `official_national_assembly_press_secondary`. Both are
official National Assembly press channels and provide strong official-hosted
corroboration for the exact code, 2015-05-15 date, Long Mỹ town creation, and
successor-district result. They are retrospective reports rather than the
enacted resolution, and neither supplies the four ward clauses or the complete
legal text.

Pháp Lý's report on the same 2019 proceedings repeats that Resolution
933/NQ-UBTVQH13 dated 2015-05-15 established Long Mỹ town and left Long Mỹ
district with 260.07 square kilometres, 84,423 residents, and eight communes:

<https://phaply.net.vn/ubtvqh-thong-qua-nghi-quyet-ve-sap-xep-cac-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-huyen-xa-a205632.html>

The article credits `congly.vn` and adds no distinct operative text.

Classification: `secondary_retrospective_legal_news`. It corroborates the
official reports but is not independent primary provenance.

## Tax-software implementation notice

CNS republishes a General Department of Taxation announcement for the
2015-08-18 HTKK 3.3.3 upgrade:

<https://cns.net.vn/htkk-3-3-3-18082015-phan-mem-ho-tro-ke-khai-to-khai-ma-vach-phien-ban-3-3-3.html>

The reproduced notice says the tax applications were updated in response to
Resolutions 931/NQ-UBTVQH13 and 933/NQ-UBTVQH13 dated 2015-05-15. For Hậu
Giang, it adds the Long Mỹ town tax sub-department and updates the application
directory with the new Long Mỹ wards and the superseded Long Mỹ district
communes.

Classification: `secondary_downstream_implementation_notice`. It confirms
that the tax administration operationalized the 2015 Long Mỹ change, but it is
a private-hosted republication and does not provide the target resolution's
text or detailed formation clauses.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO instrument key uses `2015-05-16`. All direct evidence reviewed
here identifies Resolution 933 as dated `2015-05-15`. Because no supplied
source contains the complete resolution or its effective-date clause, this
review records the discrepancy without equating the issue date to the legal
effective date or changing the pinned key.

## Registry treatment

No source-status or coverage count changes result from this review. Resolution
933 remains linked to the observed `soap:2015-04-11->2015-05-16` topology event
and remains in the primary-source-open queue.

Primary closure still requires a complete enacted text or original
PDF/DOC/scan from the National Assembly, Government, Government Gazette, VBPL,
or a Hậu Giang government archive. The new evidence substantially reduces
uncertainty about the event itself, but it cannot establish the resolution's
complete clauses, figures, signature, or effective-date provision.
