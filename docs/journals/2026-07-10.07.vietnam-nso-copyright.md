# Licensing: is NSO danh mục hành chính data free to put on Wikidata?

Written 2026-07-10. Resolves open question **#7** (licensing / CC0) raised in
`2026-07-10.05`. Not legal advice — a non-lawyer reading of the statutes, but the
statutory structure is clear enough to scope the work.

## TL;DR (decision)

**Yes — the NSO administrative-unit data is safe to incorporate into Wikidata.**
It is not "CC0" (nobody dedicated it), but that's the wrong test: the right test
for Wikidata is whether any rights *restrict reuse*, and here essentially none
do. The data is unprotectable facts, drawn from copyright-exempt legal
instruments, in two jurisdictions (Vietnam + US) that have no database right.
The only live obligation is a **statistics-law citation duty**, satisfied by
normal Wikidata referencing.

**What is NOT covered:** standalone government **photographs** (e.g. candidate
portraits, NA-session photos) are protected — relevant to `vietnam-elections-archive`,
not to this gazetteer. Keep that boundary in mind across the monorepo.

## The Vietnamese copyright framework

One consolidated statute + its implementing decree:

- **Law on Intellectual Property No. 50/2005/QH11**, amended by 36/2009/QH12,
  42/2019/QH14, and **07/2022/QH15** (16 Jun 2022, effective 1 Jan 2023).
  - **Article 14 — what's protected:** press works, photographic works,
    cinematographic works, and sketches/plans/**maps** (14.1(h)). A government
    photo is protected because it lands here and nothing exempts it.
  - **Article 15 — what's excluded:** (1) news of the day as mere items of
    information; (2) **legal instruments, administrative and judicial-domain
    documents, and their official translations**; (3) processes, systems,
    operational methods, concepts, principles and **data**. Note it defines
    exempt *document types* — it never says "the government is the author."
  - **Article 27 — terms:** photographic works = 75 yrs from publication (or 100
    from fixation if unpublished within 25 yrs); most works life + 50. (A 1946 NA
    photo is long expired; a modern one is not.)
  - **Article 39 — employer ownership:** a ministry owns works its staff/contractors create.
- **Decree No. 17/2023/ND-CP** (replacing 22/2018) interprets the above — its
  Art. 19 defines "news of the day as mere items of press information," and it
  elaborates the Art. 15 exempt categories and the Art. 14 protected ones.

Adjacent scope-definers: the **Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents**
(defines *văn bản quy phạm pháp luật* — the hierarchy the Art. 15(2) exemption
points at; 2015 version 80/2015/QH13, replaced in 2025 — verify the current
number if cited), and **Berne Art. 2(4)/2(8)** (lets states exempt official
texts and news; Vietnam took the exemption — civil-law pattern, unlike US
17 U.S.C. §105 which dumps all federal works into the public domain).

One-line version: **Article 14 protects, Article 15 exempts, Decree 17/2023
interprets.**

## Why the NSO admin-units data is free to reuse — four layers

1. **The individual facts are unprotectable everywhere that matters.** Unit
   codes, names, English names, level classifications, parent-child links,
   effective dates are facts — Art. 15(3) excludes "data" in Vietnam, and *Feist*
   does the same on the US side (where Wikimedia's servers sit). Wikidata
   statements are exactly individual referenced facts — the cleanest case.
2. **The authoritative content is itself Art. 15(2)-exempt.** Every unit's code
   and existence traces to a legal instrument (the catalog's own `Nghị định`
   column: e.g. Resolution 202/2025/QH15 of 12 Jun 2025 for most consolidated
   provinces; 30/2026/QH16, Apr 2026, for the Đồng Nai upgrade). The code system
   originates in **Decision 124/2004/QĐ-TTg** and amendments. The NSO catalog is
   a machine-readable mirror of legal normative documents — you could
   reconstruct it from the resolutions alone.
3. **No compilation or database right survives.** Compilation copyright needs
   creative selection/arrangement; an exhaustive official registry arranged in
   the legally-mandated hierarchy has neither (selection = "everything,"
   arrangement = dictated). And **neither Vietnam nor the US has an EU-style
   sui generis database right** — the thing that actually complicates Wikidata
   imports from European government data. That problem class doesn't exist here.
4. **Statistics Law is a regulatory duty, not an IP right.** The Law on
   Statistics (89/2015/QH13) requires users of official statistics to **cite the
   source and not distort the data**. It cannot attach conditions that flow
   downstream to Wikidata's CC0 output, and normal referencing satisfies it
   anyway. It mostly bears on population/area figures (statistical outputs) —
   still facts, still unprotectable.

No terms-of-use or license statement is posted on the site (typical for VN gov
portals). For creative works that absence would be a problem; for unprotectable
data it is simply neutral.

## Practical guidance for our pipeline

- **Reference every statement.** Prefer the **establishing resolution** as the
  authority, with the NSO catalog as the operational source — better provenance
  (the resolution is the authority; NSO is the registry) and it discharges the
  Statistics-Law citation duty. Concretely: `P248 stated in` the Nghị quyết +
  `reference URL`/`retrieved` for the NSO catalog.
- **Facts only.** Import codes, names, hierarchy, dates, lineage — not any
  prose/creative framing. (The `Ghi Chú` narrative is a *fact source* to parse,
  not content to copy verbatim into WD.)
- **Check current community modeling** of the 2025 34-province consolidation
  (new items vs. edited items, `replaces`/`replaced by` chains) before any batch
  write — WD's VN items are in flux (see `2026-07-10.05`).
- **Do not** ingest government **photographs** into this layer or push them to
  Commons — that's the protected category (keep separate from
  `vietnam-elections-archive`'s handling).

## Caveat

Not legal advice; VN enforcement practice can diverge from statute. But for
dataset scoping this is about as low-risk as government-data reuse gets: facts,
from copyright-exempt legal instruments, in jurisdictions with no database right.
