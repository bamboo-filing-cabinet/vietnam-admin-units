## Source-policy decision

The strict primary-source audit remains at 418 of 449 instruments with verified
official provenance. All 29 remaining change-bearing instruments have received
bounded review. The maintainer explicitly accepted that exact set as bounded
source residue so graph construction could proceed.

This decision is recorded in `data/ward-source-acceptance.json` and enforced by
the coverage builder:

- the accepted IDs must exactly equal the current change-bearing primary-source
  open set;
- each instrument retains its real `missing` or `secondary_only` status;
- no secondary source is promoted to primary evidence; and
- acceptance does not make a secondary URL automatically suitable for a
  Wikidata reference.

The generated coverage status is
`source_audit_complete_accepted_bounded_residue`. The 29 instruments remain in
the open-source checklist for later recovery.

## Graph construction

`vn_admin_units.ward_history` consumes only committed, verified artifacts:

- the 204-snapshot observed-change inventory;
- crosswalk reconciliation and component evidence;
- the accepted source-coverage ledger;
- the reviewed 2025 boundary and complete composition; and
- the small, explicit `data/ward-history-overrides.json` correction file.

The builder promotes observations into `core.Entity` records with dated name,
type, parent, and province-echo spans. It promotes only structured old-to-new
evidence into `core.LineageEdge` records. Unproved topology stays in named
residue arrays.

crosswalk rows preserve identity for 10,532 predecessor codes. Four exact

- `4070747` → `19048`, Cảnh Hóa;

- `8090727` → `29803`, Tân Quới.

One code, `24846`, is unchanged across the transition. The resulting 10,537
continued entities keep both historical and successor codes in `gso_codes`
where applicable.

The transition also exposes a genuine source defect. The 2002 SOAP roster has
two otherwise identical Xín Mần observations named Xã Ngán Chiên, codes
`2011711` and `2011715`. The crosswalk maps `2011711` to same-name successor
`01117` and leaves `2011715` base-only. The graph therefore ends `2011715`
without asserting a successor. It does not use the contaminated alternative
`2011711` → `01111`, whose successor text belongs to later Xã Trung Thịnh.

There are 214 2004 successor codes without a proved baseline identity. They are
new graph entities and explicit transition residue, not guessed continuities.

## Remaining topology residue

Outside the 2004 transition and complete 2025 reform, the retained pre-2025
crosswalk evidence proves 17 lineage edges. It does not provide a structured
old-to-new pair for every historical appearance or disappearance. The graph
therefore records:

- 454 predecessorless historical additions;
- 1,153 successorless historical removals;
- 214 transition additions without baseline identity;
- one transition predecessor without a successor; and
- source-floor anomaly code `7070901`.

These counts describe topology limits, not missing roster observations. The
graph is observation-complete across the retained snapshots and
lineage-complete for the 2025 reform. It intentionally makes no stronger claim
for earlier topology.
