# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 60/2006/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `60/2006/NĐ-CP@2006-07-04` now has two complete
commercial legal-database transcriptions, an indexed official VBPL record,
official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization metadata, and
contemporaneous Government press corroboration. It remains
primary-source-open because no complete official page or original attachment
could be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The reviewed sources identify Decree 60/2006/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2006-06-19, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc điều chỉnh địa giới hành chính xã, phường; thành lập phường thuộc thị
> xã Tân An, tỉnh Long An

The three relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-06-19`;
- official effective date: `2006-07-13`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-07-04`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL index and Ministry of Home Affairs systematization give
2006-07-13 as the legal effective date. The NSO snapshot observes the roster
changes nine days earlier. Its date remains an observation key and is not
silently rewritten.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 establishes three wards:

| Successor | Code | Legal operation | Successor total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phường 7 | `27698` | 185.09 ha and 1,475 people from An Vĩnh Ngãi; 45.65 ha and 343 people from Bình Tâm; 141.93 ha and 2,410 people from Phường 3 | 372.67 ha; 4,228 people |
| Phường Tân Khánh | `27692` | 696 ha and 5,523 people from Xã Khánh Hậu | 696 ha; 5,523 people |
| Phường Khánh Hậu | `27715` | all 388.7 ha and 5,052 people remaining in Xã Khánh Hậu | 388.7 ha; 5,052 people |

The retained units after the Phường 7 transfers have the following totals:

- Xã An Vĩnh Ngãi: 675.60 ha and 4,227 people;
- Xã Bình Tâm: 595.64 ha and 4,136 people; and
- Phường 3: 354.47 ha and 14,298 people.

The decree records the post-adjustment thị xã Tân An as 8,179.3 ha and 121,337
people, with 14 commune-level units: nine wards and five communes.

The SOAP interval `soap:2006-06-27->2006-07-04` observes Phường 7 and Phường
Tân Khánh as additions, and code `27715` changing from Xã Khánh Hậu to Phường
Khánh Hậu. This agrees exactly with crosswalk rows 51, 52, and 9479 of
`crosswalk/ward_2006-01-01_2007-01-01.xls`. The first two are partial-territory
lineage creations whose donors retain their codes; the third is preserved as
a same-code name/type change. The legal text supplies the predecessor
contributions that snapshot differencing cannot infer.

The raw SOAP rows label district code `794` as `Thành phố Tân An`, a later
parent-name echo. The decree consistently places the three operations in
`thị xã Tân An`, its legal status in 2006. The raw observation is preserved,
but the later parent label is not projected backward as legal history.

## Official-source recovery boundary

The official VBPL record is indexed at:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=15926>

The Ministry of Home Affairs' official 2024 systematization volume independently
lists the same code, title, 2006-06-19 issue date, and 2006-07-13 effective
date:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/HHTDanhMuc/Attachments/633/Tap%20HTH%202024.pdf>

These official indexed representations establish identity and dates, but they
do not satisfy the raw-artifact gate. Direct requests to the full-text, print,
Ministry of Home Affairs mirror, and properties endpoints returned HTTP 403
challenge pages. The indexed output did not expose an exact attachment
filename, so no filename is guessed or registered. The legacy
`vbpl.moj.gov.vn` hostname failed DNS resolution.

Fresh exact-code and title searches on the Government legal portal found no
matching Decree 60 record; the exact-code search returned only an unrelated
Decree 160 substring match. A year-bounded Government Gazette API search
returned no original publication PDF.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with raw retrieval blocked. Primary
closure still requires saving the complete official page or an attachment it
actually exposes, recording its source metadata and hash, and verifying its
identity and contents offline.

signature block. LuatVietnam's legacy full-text rendering has systematic
character-encoding damage, but its numerical and structural content agrees
with the clean TVPL transcription. Classification for each:
`secondary_full_text`.

## Government press corroboration

Báo Điện tử Chính phủ published a contemporaneous report on 2006-06-20:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-va-thanh-lap-them-mot-so-phuong-o-tinh-long-an-10218126.htm>

The report identifies the decree and its 2006-06-19 issue date, then reproduces
the three ward creations, every predecessor contribution, each successor
total, and the final Tân An totals. Classification:
`official_government_press_contemporaneous_enactment_corroboration`.

The report renders `diện tích tự nhiên` once as `diện tích tự nhiện` and lists
`Nhơn Thạnh Trung` as `Nhơn Thạch Trung`. Both complete legal transcriptions
use the intended forms, so these are treated as press-copy errors rather than
competing legal evidence. The report remains an official summary, not the
complete enacted instrument, and therefore does not close primary provenance.
