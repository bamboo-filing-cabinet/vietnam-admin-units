## Established identity and legal topology

The official text identifies Decree 84/2005/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2005-07-07 and signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải. Its title is the
establishment of communes in Ninh Hải district and the establishment of Thuận
Bắc district, Ninh Thuận province.

Article 1 makes five changes:

| Clause | Change | Area | Population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | Establish Thanh Hải commune from part of Nhơn Hải commune | 661.20 ha | 6,332 |
| 2 | Establish Bắc Sơn commune from part of Phương Hải commune | 6,280 ha | 5,954 |
| 3 | Transfer part of Hộ Hải commune to Tân Hải commune | 361 ha | 3,969 |
| 4 | Establish Bắc Phong commune from part of Tân Hải commune | 2,193 ha | 6,142 |
| 5 | Establish Thuận Bắc district from part of Ninh Hải district | 31,993 ha | 34,675 |

The new Thuận Bắc district contains six communes: Lợi Hải, Công Hải, Phước
Chiến, Phước Kháng, Bắc Sơn, and Bắc Phong. After the district split, Ninh Hải
retains 25,125.20 ha, 91,336 residents, and nine commune-level units: Tri Hải,
Nhơn Hải, Thanh Hải, Vĩnh Hải, Hộ Hải, Tân Hải, Xuân Hải, Phương Hải, and Khánh
Hải township.

The residual figures after the commune changes are also explicit: Nhơn Hải
retains 3,088.80 ha and 13,316 residents; Phương Hải retains 743.20 ha and
6,149 residents; Tân Hải retains 908 ha and 8,666 residents; and Hộ Hải retains
1,239 ha and 13,469 residents.

## Date discrepancy

The three dates must remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2005-07-07`;
- official effective date: `2005-07-30`; and
- pinned NSO registry/effective-event date: `2005-07-22`.

Article 2 states that the decree takes effect 15 days after Gazette
publication. VBPL's official metadata gives 2005-07-30 as the effective date,
which is also retained in the Ministry of Home Affairs' later systematization
table. The NSO key is therefore eight days earlier than the legal effective
date. This review does not rewrite the observation key or silently normalize
the discrepancy.
