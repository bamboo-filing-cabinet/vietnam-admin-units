
The portal publishes the enacted text in the page itself rather than as a
separate attachment. `ward_legal_fetch.py --assembly-recover` therefore
validates the formal code, title, adoption date, complete resolution text, and
stated effective date before archiving `.fulltext.html` as
`legal_full_text`. The eight preserved pages total 597,703 bytes.

Resolution 728 exposes a real upstream discrepancy: the official text says it
took effect on 2023-03-01, while the pinned NSO index places it at 2023-04-10.
The registry records `official_effective_date=2023-03-01` and
`effective_date_match_status=index_effective_date_differs`; it does not rewrite
the raw NSO index.

The National Assembly site's first response may be a first-party JavaScript
reload challenge. Recovery accepts only the strict 32-hex-character `D1N`
cookie shape, retries the same official URL, and then performs all content
validation. Cached artifacts remain fully offline and manifest-verified.

## Remaining official-source barrier

The official national legal database has indexed full-text pages for at least
the clustered decrees `07/NĐ-CP` through `12/NĐ-CP`, issued 2008-12-23. The
pages state an effective date of 2009-01-18, while the pinned NSO index uses
2009-01-07. That discrepancy must be retained in derived provenance exactly as
done for Resolution 728.

However, the VBPL origin currently rejects direct retrieval with its WAF, and
the browser route times out. Search-engine copies are sufficient to confirm the
lead but are not raw official artifacts, so none were promoted into the cache.
The current Government Gazette issue/search corpus also did not expose these
older originals. Official portal searches did not locate an enacted full-text
artifact for Resolution 721 or the unresolved 2015, 2017, 2020, and 2022
clusters. Editorial announcements and provincial history pages were rejected
as primary evidence.

The correct next move is to obtain raw official bytes through one of these
routes:

1. retry the VBPL full-text pages and ZIP attachments when the origin is
   accessible;
2. locate a stable official Gazette, National Assembly, provincial gazette, or
   records-library mirror for each remaining ledger ID; or
3. record an explicit policy decision accepting this bounded residue while
   leaving the source gate visibly open.

Do not promote TVPL, search result snippets, or editorial pages to official
primary evidence merely to make the count reach zero.
