new Gazette search failed to recover:

```text

08/NĐ-CP  https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12656

11/NĐ-CP  https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12652
```

The VBPL government pages identify all of 07--12/NĐ-CP as issued on
2008-12-23 and effective on 2009-01-18. The NSO index records the corresponding
rows at 2009-01-07. Treat that as a source-date discrepancy to review and record
in derived metadata; do not rewrite the pinned raw index merely to make dates
agree. Validate code, title, territory, issued/effective dates, and the attached
file before accepting each source.

The likely next implementation is a VBPL recovery provider alongside the
Gazette provider. Preserve the metadata HTML and one official original
attachment per accepted instrument. Do not promote TVPL or a search result to
primary evidence.
