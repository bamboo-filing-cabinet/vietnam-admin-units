# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 889/NQ-UBTVQH13 (Điện Bàn)

Date: 2026-09-01

The expanded Điện Bàn source bundle confirms the complete enacted scope and
effective-date clause of `889/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12`. A clean, signed
three-page scan is available from LuatVietnam, but the file is hosted by a
commercial secondary provider. No official-hosted enacted copy was recovered,
so the instrument remains `secondary_only` and primary-source-open.

This review supplements the initial three-source assessment in
`2026-08-29.03.ward-secondary-provenance-889.md`.

Exact-code and title-qualified searches of the current Government legal portal

All three pages were downloaded, rendered, and visually inspected. The scan is
complete, legible, and bears the National Assembly Chair's signature and seal.
It establishes:

- code `889/NQ-UBTVQH13` and issue date `2015-03-11`;
- the full Điện Bàn town and seven-ward title;
- Nguyễn Sinh Hùng as the signer; and
- Article 3's direction that the resolution takes effect on the date of
  signature, making the legal effective date `2015-03-11`.

Classification: `secondary_original_scan`. The document image is strong
clause-level evidence, but its LuatVietnam host is not the promulgating body or
an official legal repository. It is not archived as primary evidence and does
not close the source gate. The pinned registry key remains
`889/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12`, where `2015-03-12` is the NSO observation date,
not the legal effective date.

## Enacted administrative changes

The scan establishes Điện Bàn town from the entire former Điện Bàn district:
21,471 hectares, 229,907 people, and 20 commune-tier units. Article 1 also
establishes seven wards from the whole corresponding predecessors:
