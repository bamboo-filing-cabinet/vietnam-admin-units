# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 422/NQ-UBTVQH14

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `422/NQ-UBTVQH14@2017-08-18` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish complete secondary text
provenance and unusually strong official local implementation and historical
corroboration. No accessible source is a complete official-hosted copy of the
enacted resolution.

## Complete TVPL transcription

TVPL publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution 422/NQ-UBTVQH14:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-422-NQ-UBTVQH14-2017-dieu-chinh-mo-rong-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-thanh-pho-Thai-Nguyen-373365.aspx>

The transcription contains the formal header, 2017-08-18 date, title,
recitals, all operative and resulting-topology clauses, implementation and
effective-date clauses, distribution list, and signature block for National
Assembly Chair Nguyễn Thị Kim Ngân. Article 3 states that the resolution takes
effect on its signing date, so its issue and legal effective date are both
2017-08-18.

Article 1 transfers five complete commune-level units into Thái Nguyên city:

| Transferred unit | Former district | Area (km²) | Population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Chùa Hang township | Đồng Hỷ | 3.02 | 10,948 |
| Huống Thượng commune | Đồng Hỷ | 8.15 | 6,490 |
| Linh Sơn commune | Đồng Hỷ | 15.50 | 9,720 |
| Đồng Liên commune | Phú Bình | 8.83 | 4,977 |
| Sơn Cẩm commune | Phú Lương | 16.90 | 13,206 |

The same article establishes **Chùa Hang ward** from the entire former Chùa
Hang township and **Đồng Bẩm ward** from the entire 4.02 km² and 7,150
residents of Đồng Bẩm commune. An earlier working summary incorrectly called
the first new ward Lương Sơn; the enacted transcription establishes Chùa Hang
and Đồng Bẩm.

After the changes, the transcription gives Thái Nguyên city 222.93 km²,
362,921 residents, and 32 commune-level units: 21 wards and 11 communes. It
also gives the post-transfer totals for Đồng Hỷ district (427.73 km², 88,439
residents, 15 units), Phú Bình district (243.37 km², 144,908 residents, 20
units), and Phú Lương district (350.72 km², 94,203 residents, 15 units), and
the province-wide total of nine district-level and 180 commune-level units.

The city-unit list appears to omit punctuation between `Chùa Hang` and `Đồng
Bẩm`, but the immediately preceding establishment clauses, the stated total of
21 wards, and the remaining sources make clear that these are two ward names.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The commercial transcription supports
the complete legal identity, clauses, figures, resulting topology,
issue/effective date, and signatory, but it is not the official enacted
artifact.

The newspaper separately hosts a page specifically titled as Resolution 422:

That page advertises the resolution as two page images, which could have been
an official-local press scan of the enacted instrument. Both historical image
URLs now return HTTP 404, so their bytes and completeness cannot be verified.

Classification: `official_local_press_implementation` for the guidance page
and `official_local_scan_lead_currently_missing` for the resolution page. The
dead image references are retained as a recovery lead, not promoted to primary
evidence.

## Official local institutional consequence

The Thái Nguyên Department of Home Affairs describes the later proposal to
establish Hóa Thượng township:

<https://sonv.thainguyen.gov.vn/chinh-quyen-dia-phuong-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh/doan-cong-tac-lien-nganh-cua-trung-uong-thuc-hien-khao-sat-de-an-thanh-lap-thi-tran-hoa-thuong-huye-180507>

The official 2022 account states that Resolution 422 expanded Thái Nguyên city
and established two wards. It specifically explains that Chùa Hang township,
then the seat of Đồng Hỷ district, was transferred into Thái Nguyên city,
which led the province to locate Đồng Hỷ's new administrative offices in Hóa
Thượng commune.

Classification: `official_downstream_institutional_consequence`. It is strong
official evidence for the Chùa Hang transfer and its implementation, not the
target's enacted text.

## Official local historical corroboration

The Thái Nguyên Provincial Party portal introduces the official local party
history of Đồng Liên commune:

<https://thainguyen.dcs.vn/van-kien-tu-lieu/lich-su-dang-bo-tp-thai-nguyen/lich-su-dang-bo-xa-dong-lien-1947-2020-180.html>

The page states that Đồng Liên previously belonged to Phú Bình district and
that Resolution 422, dated 2017-08-18, transferred the commune into Thái
Nguyên city. It also provides the complete 324-page local history as a PDF.

Classification: `official_local_historical_corroboration`. This independently
supports one of the five whole-unit transfers, but is a later history rather
than the resolution itself.

## State legal-press announcement

Báo Pháp Luật reports the October 2017 ceremony at which National Assembly
Vice Chair Đỗ Bá Tỵ formally presented Resolution 422 to local leaders:

<https://baophapluat.vn/thai-nguyen-long-trong-don-nhan-huan-chuong-lao-dong-hang-nhat-post260603.html>

The article says the city implemented Resolution 422 to expand its boundary
and establish two wards. Deputy Prime Minister Phạm Bình Minh and other
central and local officials attended. It does not reproduce the resolution's
operative clauses or detailed figures.

Classification: `state_legal_press_contemporaneous_implementation`. It is
contemporaneous state-press corroboration of formal promulgation and
implementation, not an enacted copy.

## Official-original search and date treatment

An exact-code search of the Government Gazette did not recover Resolution 422,
and inspection of the surrounding August-October 2017 resolution listings did
not expose it. This is a bounded search result, not evidence that no official
copy exists elsewhere.

The NSO instrument key, the complete TVPL transcription, Báo Thái Nguyên, the
Department of Home Affairs, and the Đồng Liên history all identify 2017-08-18.
The transcribed Article 3 makes that signing date the effective date, so this
review reveals no issue/effective/observation-date discrepancy.

## Registry treatment

`data/ward-legal-secondary-urls.json` now records the TVPL identity URL, so the
deterministic legal registry and coverage ledger classify Resolution 422 as
`secondary_only` instead of `missing`. This improves the evidence description
but does not change the official-source count or remove the instrument from the
primary-source-open queue.

Primary closure still requires a complete enacted text or original
PDF/DOC/scan from the National Assembly, Government, Government Gazette, VBPL,
or an authoritative Thái Nguyên archive. Recovering the two missing Báo Thái
Nguyên page images would be especially useful if their provenance and complete
