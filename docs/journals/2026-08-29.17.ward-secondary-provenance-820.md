# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 820/NQ-UBTVQH14

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `820/NQ-UBTVQH14@2020-01-01` now has strong,
mutually consistent secondary provenance from National Assembly, provincial,
district, and state-press sources. It remains primary-source-open because no
reviewed source is a complete official-hosted enacted text or original
PDF/DOC/scan of Resolution 820 itself.

## Established identity and topology

The sources consistently identify Resolution 820/NQ-UBTVQH14 as issued by the
National Assembly Standing Committee on 2019-11-21, titled as the arrangement
of commune-level administrative units in Bình Thuận province, and effective
from 2020-01-01.

They establish three whole-unit combinations:

| Former units | Resulting unit | District |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hòa Phú commune + Phan Rí Cửa township | Phan Rí Cửa township | Tuy Phong |
| Đức Tân commune + Măng Tố commune | Măng Tố commune | Tánh Linh |
| Đức Chính commune + Nam Chính commune | Nam Chính commune | Đức Linh |

The province consequently decreased from 127 to 124 commune-level units. The
post-change total was 93 communes, 19 wards, and 12 townships across ten
district-level units: eight districts, one town, and one city.

## Contemporaneous official Tuy Phong implementation

The Tuy Phong district portal reports the 2020-01-02 proclamation ceremony:

<https://tuyphong.binhthuan.gov.vn/thi-tran-phan-ri-cua-66973/le-cong-bo-sap-nhap-hoa-phu-va-thi-tran-phan-ri-cua-huyen-tuy-phong-566787>

The article names the resolution, issue date, title, and 2020-01-01 effective
date. It lists all three combinations and the province-wide post-change
totals. For its local branch, it gives the resulting Phan Rí Cửa township an
area of 14.94 km² and population of 45,805, and gives Tuy Phong district 11
commune-level units: nine communes and two townships.

Classification: `official_local_contemporaneous_implementation`. This is
excellent evidence that the resolution was proclaimed and implemented as
described, but the article does not reproduce the complete enacted text or
offer an original attachment.

The current HTTPS certificate chain for the historical Bình Thuận district
host is incomplete in the command-line client. The page remains retrievable
when certificate verification is relaxed. That access condition does not
convert the page into an enacted artifact or justify archiving it as primary.

## Downstream provincial decision

TVPL publishes the complete text of Bình Thuận Provincial People's Committee
Decision 3361/QĐ-UBND dated 2019-12-27:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Quyet-dinh-3361-QD-UBND-2019-chuyen-thon-khu-pho-tai-cac-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-xa-Binh-Thuan-434928.aspx>

The decision expressly cites Resolution 820 and translates the three
arrangements into village and neighborhood administration:

- four Hòa Phú villages became neighborhoods of the new Phan Rí Cửa township;
- five Đức Tân villages became villages 4 through 8 of the new Măng Tố
  commune; and
- four Đức Chính villages became villages 7 through 10 of the new Nam Chính
  commune.

Decision 3361 also took effect on 2020-01-01. It is a downstream provincial
implementation instrument rather than a copy of Resolution 820.

Classification: `secondary_downstream_legal_text`. This TVPL URL is retained
in the deterministic secondary-source map because it supplies an explicit
legal cross-reference and detailed implementation evidence. Its registry
presence must not be read as an exact transcription of the target resolution.

## Official Đức Linh and successor-site evidence

The historical Đức Linh district portal reports the 2020-01-02 proclamation
of the Nam Chính/Đức Chính combination:

<https://duclinh.binhthuan.gov.vn/chinh-tri-xa-hoi/cong-bo-sap-nhap-xa-duc-chinh-va-nam-chinh-567337>

It identifies Resolution 820 and states that the resulting Nam Chính commune
had 51.69 km² and 15,379 people. It also reports the same four village changes
later formalized by Decision 3361.

The former Nam Chính commune introduction independently records the
combination, effective date, resulting area, population, boundaries, and ten
villages:

<https://duclinh.binhthuan.gov.vn/xa-nam-chinh/gioi-thieu-chung-xa-nam-chinh-568643>

After the 2025 provincial reorganization, an official Lâm Đồng successor-site
history preserves the earlier Resolution 820 event:

<https://duclinh.lamdong.gov.vn/gioi-thieu-chung/gioi-thieu-chung-565162>

It states that all 23.75 km² and 5,770 people of Đức Chính were incorporated
into Nam Chính, producing the same 51.69 km² and 15,379-person result.

Classification: `official_local_implementation_and_historical_corroboration`.
The three pages strongly support the Nam Chính branch but none is the enacted
resolution.

## National Assembly oversight evidence

The National Assembly portal reports a later Bình Thuận delegation oversight
visit concerning the 2019-2021 administrative-unit arrangements:

<https://quochoi.vn/tintuc/Pages/tin-doan-dai-bieu-quoc-hoi.aspx?ItemID=61113>

The article states that Đức Linh implemented Resolution 820 by combining Đức
Chính and Nam Chính into the new Nam Chính commune, operating from 2020-01-01.
It records the delegation's finding that the arrangement was implemented in a
timely and lawful manner and discusses later staffing and public-service
effects.

Classification: `official_national_assembly_downstream_oversight`. The host is
the issuing institution, but this item is an oversight news article rather
than the target instrument's formal text.

## Official-original search

A fresh exact-code search of the Government legal portal returned no result.
A fresh Government Gazette query returned similar administrative-arrangement
resolutions, including neighboring 813, 814, 816, 827, 828, 830, and 834, but
not Resolution 820.

The Vietlaw National Assembly catalog was also checked directly. Its Standing
Committee list page 111 contains the 2019-11-21 sequence 815, 816, 817, 818,
and 819, but no 820; page 112 continues with 812 and 814. A simple Vietlaw
search for `820` returned zero records. The otherwise-unused adjacent item IDs
28359-28361 and 28363-28365 resolve to unrelated National Assembly
resolutions, so they do not conceal the missing record.

These are bounded negative findings. They explain why the official artifact
was not promoted, but do not establish that no official copy exists in an
unindexed archive.

## Registry treatment

`data/ward-legal-secondary-urls.json` now records the TVPL Decision 3361 page
as a downstream legal cross-reference. Regenerating the registry and coverage
ledger reclassifies Resolution 820 from `missing` to `secondary_only`; it does
not change the official-source count or remove the instrument from the
primary-source-open queue.

Primary closure still requires the complete enacted text or original
PDF/DOC/scan of Resolution 820 from the National Assembly, Government,
Government Gazette, VBPL, or an authoritative Bình Thuận archive.
