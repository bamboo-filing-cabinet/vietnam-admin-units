# How Wikidata models the 2025 reform: rename vs. new item

Written 2026-07-10. Research input for the **open** identity-model question
(deliberately not yet decided). Prompted by: when old *phường Trúc Bạch* becomes
new *phường Ba Đình* (đổi tên + hợp nhất), does Wikidata rename the item or make
a new one? The answer should drive our model, since Goal B is to feed Wikidata.

## Wikidata's general convention

An item's identity is its **Q-number**, independent of its label. Labels/aliases
are mutable, multilingual, and are **not statements**. So:

- **Pure rename (same real-world entity, new name):** edit the item's label,
  keep the old name as an **alias** (or `P1448 official name` with start/end
  qualifiers). The **Q-id is stable** — one item across the rename.
- **Merger / new legal entity:** community practice is to **create a new item**,
  and ideally link with `P1365 replaces` / `P1366 replaced by`, `P571 inception`,
  `P576 dissolved`. This is judgment-dependent and varies.

| P571 inception | — | **— (missing)** |
| P576 dissolved | **— (missing)** | — |
| P1365 replaces | — | **— (missing)** |
| P1366 replaced by | **— (missing)** | — |

**Findings:**

1. The community **created a new item** (Q135651473) for the new ward — it did
   **not** rename the old Trúc Bạch item. So this was treated as a *new entity*,
   not a relabel.
2. This is correct framing: it's not a pure rename — the crosswalk says *"Đổi
   tên từ phường Trúc Bạch thành phường Ba Đình, **hợp nhất** phường Quán Thánh,
   …"* — a rename **+ merge of ~10 wards**, i.e. a new legal unit. "Merger → new
   item" fits the general convention.
3. **The migration is incomplete.** The new item has no `P571`, no `P1365`; the
   old item has no `P576`, no `P1366`, and its `P131` still points at the
   abolished Ba Đình *district*. There is **no lineage link** between them. This
   is the "Wikidata is mid-migration" state the `vietnam-elections-wikidata`
   lao-cai files describe — now confirmed at ward level with a clean example.

## Implication for our identity model (still open, now informed)

Wikidata's de-facto choice: **one entity per reform-generation unit**, linked to
predecessors — not a single item carried through the merge. That matches the
`vietnam-elections-wikidata` electoral-unit precedent ("new item on namespace
redraw; persist within a stable namespace") one layer down. So the leaning:

- **Reform / merger / split → a NEW entity** (new `local_id`, new WD item),
  linked by lineage to its predecessor(s).
- **Pure rename or minor boundary tweak within a stable period → SAME entity**
  (relabel; old name as alias).

**The genuine tension to resolve** (why it stays open): the crosswalk gives a
"primary" continuity signal — new Ba Đình inherits Trúc Bạch's **code `00004`**
and is literally *"đổi tên từ Trúc Bạch"*. A *continuity* reading would keep
Trúc Bạch's item, rename it, and merge the others into it (fewer items, keeps
history on one node). Wikidata's community instead chose the *new-entity*
reading. Both are defensible; picking ours is the open decision. Leaning toward
matching the community (new entity + lineage), because Goal B is to feed
Wikidata and diverging would fight the existing items.

## What this makes Goal B concretely

The community created new items but **did not do the linking**. So our Wikidata
correction work is largely:

- **New units:** set `P571` (inception, e.g. 2025-07-01), `P1365 replaces` →
  predecessor(s), verify `P131`/`P31`. (Many new items already exist, e.g.
  Q135651473 — we enrich, not create, where present.)
- **Old units:** set `P576` (dissolved 2025-07-01), `P1366 replaced by` →
  successor, keep `P131` as historical. Fix stale `P31` where wrong.
- The predecessor→successor edges come from our lineage layer (crosswalk primary
  link + parsed `Ghi Chú` for the full merge set).

This is a tractable, high-value contribution: the items exist; the **graph
structure connecting them is missing**, and that's exactly what our lineage
layer produces.
