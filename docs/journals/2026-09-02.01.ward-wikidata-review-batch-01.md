## Durable review record

`data/ward-wikidata-review-decisions.json` is the machine-readable review
ledger. It records the batch selection, candidates compared, canonical ward
predecessors, normalized live Wikidata evidence, Vietnamese Wikipedia page
bindings and extracts, the decision rationale, and the retrieval time.

The mapper applies that ledger after the automatic QLever and Action API
passes. Assigned rows use `match_status=manual`; a future reviewed row that
cannot be resolved can use `reviewed-unresolved`. This keeps the review queue
moving without weakening the automatic matcher or hiding an unresolved
identity. The audit checks that every ledger decision is reflected in the CSV
and continues to reject assigned-QID collisions.
