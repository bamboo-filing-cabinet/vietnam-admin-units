## Guardrails

- Do not count TVPL, LuatVietnam, news articles, search snippets, or a later
  implementation decision as the target's primary enacted artifact.
- An official-domain news or history page is valuable corroboration but closes
  the gate only if it embeds the complete enacted text or authenticated complete
  original scans.
- Retain issue/effective/observation-date discrepancies explicitly.
- Verify title, territory, date, and named units; resolution numbers are reused.
- Keep secondary evidence in dated journals even when it does not lower the
  open count.
- Commit and push the submodule first, then commit and push the monorepo pointer.
