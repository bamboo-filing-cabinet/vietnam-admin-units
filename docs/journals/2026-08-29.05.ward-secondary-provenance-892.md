# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 892/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

`892/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12` remains primary-source-open. The supplied
sources and a complete commercial transcription establish strong secondary
provenance, including independent official and state-press corroboration, but
none supplies the complete 2015 resolution from an official host.

## Complete LuatVietnam transcription

LuatVietnam publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution
892/NQ-UBTVQH13:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/dia-gioi-hanh-chinh/nghi-quyet-892-nq-ubtvqh13-2015-thanh-lap-phuong-xuat-hoa-huyen-tung-tinh-bac-kan-272497-d1.html>

The transcription contains the formal header and 2015-03-11 issue date,
resolution title, recitals, all operative clauses, implementation clause,
effective-date clause, distribution list, and signature block for National
Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Article 1 establishes:

- Xuất Hóa ward from the entire former Xuất Hóa commune: 4,804.9 hectares and
  3,461 residents;
- Huyền Tụng ward from the entire former Huyền Tụng commune: 2,735.6 hectares
  and 4,769 residents; and
- Bắc Kạn city from the entire former Bắc Kạn town: 13,688 hectares, 56,818
  residents, and eight commune-level units.

After the changes, Bắc Kạn city comprises six wards and two communes. Article
3 states that the resolution takes effect from the date of signing, placing
both issue and effectiveness on 2015-03-11.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The transcription is complete enough
to support the resolution's identity, clauses, date, and signatory, but it is
commercially hosted rather than an official original artifact.

### Nhân Dân anniversary article

<https://nhandan.vn/ky-niem-10-nam-thanh-lap-thanh-pho-bac-kan-post863746.html>

The state newspaper's 2025 anniversary report says that on 2015-03-11 the
Standing Committee of the National Assembly issued Resolution
892/NQ-UBTVQH13, converting Xuất Hóa and Huyền Tụng communes into wards and
Bắc Kạn town into Bắc Kạn city.

Classification: `state_press_secondary`.

### Ministry of Construction planning page

<https://quyhoach.xaydung.gov.vn/vn/quy-hoach/10011/do-an-quy-hoach-chi-tiet-ty-le-1-500-khu-do-thi-nha-o-dan-cu-thu-nhap-thap.aspx>

The Ministry of Construction planning portal's introductory history records
the 2015-03-11 issuance of Resolution 892/NQ-UBTVQH13 and the establishment of
Xuất Hóa and Huyền Tụng wards. This is an official central-government host, but
the statement is contextual planning-page prose rather than the resolution
itself.

Classification: `official_secondary`.

The page's attached PDF is:

<https://quyhoach.xaydung.gov.vn/Images/Quyhoach/fileDK/bf56db77-4704-4eb3-81ed-5b71b9b9e1cf_bk-kdcto4ducxuan_2637-qd-ubnd-28-12-2018.pdf>

The six-page signed and stamped scan is Bắc Kạn city People's Committee
Decision 2637/QĐ-UBND dated 2018-12-28, approving a 1:500 detailed plan for a
low-income residential urban area. Visual review of all six pages found no
reference to Resolution 892/NQ-UBTVQH13. It therefore supplies no direct
provenance for the 2015 resolution.

Classification: `unrelated_attachment`.

### Government portal false match

## Registry treatment

The pinned NSO key remains `892/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12`. The complete
transcription and independent corroborating sources place issuance on
2015-03-11, and the take-effect-on-signing clause makes that the transcribed
effective date as well. The one-day discrepancy is preserved rather than
normalized away.

No source-status or coverage count changes result from this review. The TVPL
URL was already counted as secondary evidence; the stronger full text and
official/state-press corroboration are preserved in this journal. Primary
closure still requires a complete enacted text or original PDF/DOC from an
official National Assembly, Government, VBPL, Gazette, or Bắc Kạn government
host.
