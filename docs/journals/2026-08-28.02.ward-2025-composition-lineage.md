## The source run is 34 instruments, not 32

The first inventory started at `1656/NQ-UBTVQH15`. Cross-checking the legal
index against successor province codes exposed two earlier instruments:

- `1654/NQ-UBTVQH15` — An Giang; and
- `1655/NQ-UBTVQH15` — Cà Mau.

The complete consecutive run is therefore `1654`–`1687/NQ-UBTVQH15`, one
instrument for each of the 34 post-reform provinces/cities. The fetcher rejects
any legal index that does not contain that exact sequence.

For every instrument the raw cache preserves two official artifacts:

1. the signed PDF from `datafiles.chinhphu.vn`, retained as the authority; and
2. the matching full-text page from the Government Newspaper at
   `xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn`, used as the machine-readable transcription.

The 34 PDFs occupy 16,201,336 bytes and the 34 HTML pages 18,025,124 bytes.
Every manifest row records its document code, effective date, source URL, hash,
and, for HTML, the matching signed-PDF path/URL. The fetcher validates PDF
magic bytes, UTF-8 HTML, one official article body, the requested resolution
code, and Article 1 arrangement text. A verified local pair is never fetched
again.

Representative official records:

- [signed Hanoi resolution 1656](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2025/6/1656-nq.signed.pdf);
- [official Hanoi full text](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-quyet-so-1656-nq-ubtvqh15-sap-xep-cac-dvhc-cap-xa-cua-thanh-pho-ha-noi-nam-2025-119250616192433872.htm); and
- [official Tây Ninh full text](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-quyet-so-1682-nq-ubtvqh15-sap-xep-cac-dvhc-cap-xa-cua-tinh-tay-ninh-nam-2025-119250616210257367.htm).

## Clause extraction and successor coverage

`ward_composition.extract_resolution_clauses` reads only hash-verified HTML,
limits parsing to the official article body, stops at Article 2, and maps each
numbered Article 1 arrangement clause to exactly one 2025-07-01 successor in
the resolution's province. Target matching tolerates HTML spacing defects and
Vietnamese tone-placement variants but rejects a missing or non-unique target.

This yields 3,194 distinct clause targets. The remaining 127 of the 3,321
successors must have an NSO note that explicitly says the unit is unchanged;
anything else fails the build. Thus:

```text
3,194 arranged successors + 127 unchanged successors = 3,321
```

The five post units with no structured ward-tier base remain the only units
without incoming ward edges: Bạch Long Vĩ, Cồn Cỏ, Hoàng Sa, Lý Sơn, and Côn
Đảo. Whole-former-district clauses, such as Vân Đồn, expand from the preserved
2025-06-30 district parent and do not invent a commune-tier base for those five
creations.

## Constituent matching and why NSO result names are not parsed as edges

The legal-clause matcher works within the successor province's old-province
scope and matches against the 10,035 pre-reform SOAP observations. It uses:

- explicit or list-carried unit tier;
- literal Vietnamese spelling before folded fallback;
- former-district names where duplicate ward names require disambiguation;
- the structured primary code as a final disambiguator;
- result-clause removal before constituent matching; and
- guards for legal vocabulary and numerals (`diện tích tự nhiên`, `hợp nhất`,
  and `1 phần` must not become wards named Tự Nhiên, Hợp Nhất, or Phường 1).

An adversarial audit caught why the NSO prose cannot simply be unioned into the
graph. Parsing an entire note allowed a predecessor named Hòa Bình to match a
new result named Hòa Bình; `Yên Hợp` could span `Bảo Yên, hợp nhất`; and numeric
ward names could match `1 phần`. The final build therefore uses resolution
clauses as constituent evidence and uses NSO notes only to classify the 127
unchanged units. The NSO notes remain an independent review source, not an
automatic source of extra edges.

Known false-positive regression gates now assert, among others:

- old `04426` Yên Hợp resolves only to `04441`, not every clause containing
  “Yên, hợp nhất”;
- old `04825` Hòa Bình resolves only to `04792`, not successors whose result
  name mentions Hòa Bình;
- old `08014` Hợp Nhất resolves only to `07969`;
- old `10219` Tự Nhiên resolves only to `10237`; and
- old `27247` Phường 1 is not matched from the quantity phrase `1 phần`.

## Explicit curation and drift gate

`data/ward-2025-composition-overrides.json` records identities that the strict
parser must not guess. Its 192 predecessors / 195 edges comprise:

- 179 previously unresolved predecessor identities producing 182 edges; and
- 13 supplemental split edges for predecessors that already had another
  successor and therefore could not appear in the unresolved set.

These cover official HTML spacing defects (`vàTúng Sánthành`), typography or
spelling variants (`EaBia`/`Ea Bia`, `Đak`/`Đăk`, `Hoà`/`Hòa`), Roman/numeric
ward labels (`Phường IV` → old `Phường 4`), same-name wards requiring former
district context, and parser-resistant remainder/split clauses. Every curated
target must be an extracted legal clause in the predecessor's allowed province
scope.

The curation is executable residue, not a loose override:

- its primary key set must equal the unresolved-before-curation predecessor
  set exactly;
- a supplemental predecessor must already be resolved, but its supplemental
  edge must not already exist;
- no predecessor can appear in both sections;
- empty/duplicate successor lists fail; and
- the generated artifact records the curation file's SHA-256.

Examples locked by tests include Hòa Bình `10798` splitting to `10804` and
`10843`, EaBia `22228` splitting to `22207` and `22222`, Hải Hòa/Hải Lạng
cross-splits, and Tây Ninh old Phường 4 `25462` contributing to Tân Ninh
`25459`.

## Completeness and topology gates

The build fails unless all of the following hold:

- all 34 PDF/HTML pairs hash-verify;
- all 3,194 legal targets are unique and the 127-unit unchanged complement is
  exact;
- all 10,035 pre-reform codes have at least one successor edge;
- every one of the 3,316 structured primary links exists in the final edge set;
- the five blank-base creations are exactly the successors without incoming
  ward edges;
- every edge carries source evidence;
- `share=partial` if and only if the predecessor outdegree exceeds one; and
- two independent topology totals agree:
  `edges - predecessors = partial_edges - split_predecessors = 551`.

Ground-truth coverage includes all 12 former Vân Đồn commune-tier units, the
Hanoi Ba Đình/Hoàn Kiếm/Cửa Nam splits, Hải Hòa and Hải Lạng, both curated
two-way splits above, and `Phường IV`/Phường 4 → Tân Ninh.

The artifact retains 382 ambiguous lexical parser mentions and 98 clauses in
which the strict phrase matcher did not rediscover the structured primary name.
These are diagnostics, not unresolved topology: the primary edge gate covers
all 98, the curation drift gate covers parser-resistant identities/splits, and
the accepted edge set has zero predecessor residue. Diagnostics remain in the
artifact so parser changes can be audited instead of silently changing data.

Full verification result: **163 passed**. The composition writer is also tested
for byte-identical repeated output.
