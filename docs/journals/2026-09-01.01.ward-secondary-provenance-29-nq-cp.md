## Enacted administrative changes

The supplied sources and existing complete secondary transcription establish
that Resolution 29:

- transfers 1,645.25 hectares and 575 people from xã Phú Mỹ, huyện Kiên
  Lương, to phường Đông Hồ, thị xã Hà Tiên;
- establishes xã Tân Hòa in Tân Hiệp from 3,524.87 hectares and 8,566 people
  of xã Tân Hiệp B;
- establishes xã Thạnh Bình in Giồng Riềng from 1,918.08 hectares and 7,596
  people of xã Thạnh Hòa;
- establishes xã Bình Minh in Vĩnh Thuận from 3,095.54 hectares and 6,400
  people of xã Vĩnh Bình Nam; and
- establishes huyện Giang Thành from 40,744.3 hectares and 28,910 people of
  Kiên Lương, comprising Phú Mỹ, Phú Lợi, Tân Khánh Hòa, Vĩnh Điều, and Vĩnh
  Phú communes.

## Supplied-source assessment

- Báo Chính phủ published a contemporaneous summary on `2009-06-30`. It
  identifies Resolutions 28 and 29, reports the Phú Mỹ→Đông Hồ transfer, all
  three commune establishments, and the area and population used to establish
  Giang Thành. It is official press corroboration, not the enacted resolution:
  <https://baochinhphu.vn/dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-tai-2-tinh-tien-giang-va-kien-giang-10214378.htm>
- Sài Gòn Giải Phóng reported the `2009-09-02` implementation ceremony. It
  confirms Resolution 29, Giang Thành's enacted area and population, and its
  five-commune roster. The later ceremony date is implementation context, not
  the legal effective date:
  <https://www.sggp.org.vn/kien-giang-thanh-lap-huyen-giang-thanh-post184960.html>
- Thư Viện Pháp Luật remains the deterministic complete secondary identity and
