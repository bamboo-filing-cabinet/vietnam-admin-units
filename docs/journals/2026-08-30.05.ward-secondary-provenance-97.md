# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 97/2005/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-30

The pinned NSO instrument `97/2005/NĐ-CP@2005-08-10` now has a complete
indexed official VBPL print view, an official effective-history record, an
exact original-DOC path, two complete commercial transcriptions, and three
independent corroborating sources supplied by the maintainer. It remains

verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The official record identifies Decree 97/2005/NĐ-CP as issued by the
Government on 2005-07-26, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc thành lập thành phố Rạch Giá thuộc tỉnh Kiên Giang; điều chỉnh địa
> giới hành chính, thành lập xã thuộc các huyện Gò Quao, An Minh, Châu Thành,
> Kiên Hải và Giồng Riềng, tỉnh Kiên Giang

The three relevant dates must remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2005-07-26`;
- official effective date: `2005-08-17`; and
- pinned NSO registry/effective-event date: `2005-08-10`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. VBPL's
official history records 2005-08-17 as the effective date. The NSO snapshot
therefore reflects the five new commune-level units seven days before legal
effectiveness. The registry date remains an observation key and is not
silently rewritten.

The raw NSO title says `Anh Minh` and `Giồng Giềng`. The official text says
`An Minh` and `Giồng Riềng`. These are source spellings to preserve, not a
reason to alter the raw legal-index row.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 contains one district-tier retype, one boundary transfer, and five
commune creations:

| Operation | Source | Result | Area transferred | Population transferred |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| District-tier retype | thị xã Rạch Giá | thành phố Rạch Giá | 9,775.42 ha total | 205,660 total |
| Boundary transfer | Vĩnh Phước B | thị trấn Gò Quao | 280 ha | 1,230 |
| Commune creation | Đông Thạnh | Tân Thạnh | 3,956 ha | 10,939 |
| Commune creation | Mong Thọ B | Mong Thọ | 1,480.42 ha | 7,938 |
| Commune creation | An Sơn | Nam Du | 440 ha | 5,484 |
| Commune creation | Hòa Thuận | Ngọc Hòa | 3,009.68 ha | 11,170 |
| Commune creation | Vĩnh Thạnh | Vĩnh Phú | 2,730 ha | 4,968 |

The Rạch Giá retype retains its existing 12 subordinate units: eleven wards
and Phi Thông commune. The Gò Quao operation changes boundaries but creates or
dissolves no commune-level unit. Each source commune for the five creations
survives with a reduced area and population. The decree therefore produces a
net increase of five commune-level units.

The `soap:2005-07-22->2005-08-10` observation contains nine additions. The
five Kiên Giang additions are Mong Thọ (`30887`), Vĩnh Phú (`30947`), Ngọc Hoà
(`30950`), Tân Thạnh (`31031`), and Nam Du (`31115`); each has a crosswalk row
naming Decree 97. The other four additions and the Phụng Hiệp changes belong
to Decree 98/2005/NĐ-CP. The official spelling `Ngọc Hòa` and SOAP spelling
`Ngọc Hoà` normalize to the same identity.

## Complete commercial transcriptions

Thư Viện Pháp Luật publishes the complete text:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bat-dong-san/Nghi-dinh-97-2005-ND-CP-thanh-lap-Rach-Gia-thuoc-tinh-Kien-Giang-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-xa-thuoc-huyen-Go-Quao-An-Minh-Chau-Thanh-Kien-Hai-2566.aspx>

It contains the formal identity, recitals, city retype, boundary transfer, all
five creations, areas, populations, boundary descriptions, effective clause,
implementation clause, and signature block. Its operative text agrees with
the official print view.

LuatVietnam also publishes the complete text:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/linh-vuc-khac/nghi-dinh-97-2005-nd-cp-chinh-phu-17588-d1.html>

Its topology, areas, populations, and remaining-unit totals agree with the
official print and TVPL copies, and its metadata gives the correct 2005-08-17
effective date. The indexed rendering has presentation defects—among them
`Xà`, split `nhâ n`, and dropped `Nam` labels in several directional boundary
sentences—but the official print resolves those omissions. No substantive
topology disagreement remains.

Classification: both pages are `secondary_full_text`; neither is an archived
official enacted artifact. The TVPL URL was already retained in
`data/decree-urls.json`, so this review does not add or replace a deterministic
secondary URL.

## Contemporaneous and retrospective corroboration

Tiền Phong published a contemporaneous report on 2005-07-28:

<https://tienphong.vn/kien-giang-thanh-lap-thanh-pho-rach-gia-post17133.tpo>

It identifies Decree 97, gives Rạch Giá's area, population, and 12-unit roster,
records the 280 ha/1,230-person Gò Quao transfer, and names all five new
communes. Classification:
`state_press_contemporaneous_topology_corroboration`. It is a detailed
contemporaneous summary, not enacted text.

Thời Báo Ngân Hàng published a later report on 2014-02-19:

<https://thoibaonganhang.vn/tp-rach-gia-duoc-cong-nhan-la-do-thi-loai-ii-8448.html>

The report says Rạch Giá was established under Decree 97/2005/NĐ-CP dated
2005-07-26. Classification: `state_press_retrospective_identity_corroboration`.
It corroborates the city retype only and does not cover the commune topology.

The Ministry of Construction planning portal states that Rạch Giá was
recognized as a provincial city under Decree 97 dated 2005-07-26:

<https://quyhoach.xaydung.gov.vn/vn/quy-hoach/3131/dieu-chinh-quy-hoach-chung-xay-dung-thanh-pho-rach-gia--tinh-kien-giang-den-nam-2025--ty-le-1-5000.aspx>

Classification: `official_retrospective_city_identity_corroboration`. It is an
official planning-context page, not the original legal instrument. The live
host presented a certificate-name mismatch during review; the page was
readable only after bypassing certificate verification, so it is not used as a
raw-cache source.
