# Ward primary-source recovery: Resolution 904/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

## Result

`904/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-04-11` now has complete primary provenance from the
Ninh Bình Provincial Historical Archives:

<https://luutru.ninhbinh.gov.vn/gioi-thieu-tai-lieu-luu-tru/lich-su-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-thanh-pho-tam-diep-qua-tai-lieu-luu-tru-213.html>

The official archive history identifies Resolution 904/NQ-UBTVQH13, its
2015-04-10 date, the establishment of Yên Bình ward, and the elevation of Tam
Điệp town to Tam Điệp city. More importantly, the page embeds the complete
two-page signed resolution from archival file `HSNT_2015_026_002`:

- <https://luutru.ninhbinh.gov.vn/uploads/images/anh_bai_viet/Gi%E1%BB%9Bi_thi%E1%BB%87u_TLLT/TamDiep/6-_NQ_904-NQ-UBTVQH13_(HSNT_2015_026_002)_Page1.png>; and
- <https://luutru.ninhbinh.gov.vn/uploads/images/anh_bai_viet/Gi%E1%BB%9Bi_thi%E1%BB%87u_TLLT/TamDiep/6-2_NQ_904-NQ-UBTVQH13_(HSNT_2015_026_002)_Page2.png>.

The scans preserve the formal header, code, date, title, recitals, all three
operative articles, distribution list, National Assembly seal, and Nguyễn
Sinh Hùng signature. They therefore close the primary-source requirement; the
archive history page alone would have remained an official derivative summary.

## Enacted changes

Article 1 establishes Yên Bình ward from the full 459.8 hectares and 7,486
residents of Yên Bình commune. Article 2 establishes Tam Điệp city from the
full 10,497.9 hectares, 104,175 residents, and nine commune-level units of Tam
Điệp town. The resulting city comprises six wards—Bắc Sơn, Nam Sơn, Tân Bình,
Tây Sơn, Trung Sơn, and Yên Bình—and three communes—Đông Sơn, Quang Sơn, and
Yên Sơn.

Article 3 states that the resolution takes effect from the date of signing.
The official issue and effective date is therefore 2015-04-10.

### Địa Ốc Thông Thái

The private real-estate/geography page provides a general Ninh Bình district
history:

<https://diaocthongthai.com/danh-sach-cac-huyen-ninh-binh/>

Classification: `private_tertiary`. Its Tam Điệp passage gives 2015-05-01 as
the effective date and 103,836 as the population. Those values conflict with
the signed resolution's take-effect-on-signing date of 2015-04-10 and
population of 104,175. Its 104.97-square-kilometre area is only a rounded
version of the official 10,497.9 hectares. It is not used for provenance or
legal figures.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO legal index uses `2015-04-11`. Both signed scan pages and the
official archive history use `2015-04-10`, while Article 3 makes that signing
date the legal effective date. The registry retains the NSO date in the stable
instrument key and records the official date separately:

```text
effective_date=2015-04-11
issued_date=2015-04-10
official_effective_date=2015-04-10
effective_gap_days=1
date_match_status=plausible_effective_lag
effective_date_match_status=index_effective_date_differs
```

No upstream value was rewritten or silently normalized.

## Reproducible recovery

`ward_legal_fetch.py --archive-recover` now supports curated official
provincial archive pages. For Resolution 904 it:

1. validates the exact code, archive identifier, date, and title-bearing
   history sentence;
2. extracts exactly two scan URLs and validates their page order;
3. allowlists every official HTTPS URL;
4. downloads both originals and verifies their PNG signatures; and
5. verifies the HTML and scan bytes against the raw manifest offline.

Commands:

```sh
uv run python -m vn_admin_units.ward_legal_fetch --archive-recover
uv run python -m vn_admin_units.ward_legal_fetch --fetch
uv run python -m vn_admin_units.ward_legal_fetch --check
```
