# Immediate ward predecessor reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Result

The 10,035 distinct immediate predecessors in the 2025 ward reform now have a
complete reviewed disposition:

- 6,100 were assigned automatically from the saved ward-class candidate corpus;
- 54 more were assigned by unrestricted exact-name discovery;
- two final identities were assigned manually;
- 3,879 are provisional former-item creation gaps awaiting sampled audit.

The mapping therefore has 6,156 predecessor QIDs, and 6,479 of 10,586 reform
edges currently have both endpoint QIDs. The lineage emitter remains correctly
blocked on the 3,879 creation gaps.

## Candidate reduction

`ward_reconcile_predecessors` indexed the existing 11,838-item QLever ward
corpus once by folded name, excluded all 3,321 current-unit QIDs, and matched
candidate parents against terminal district identities. District code aliases
are augmented only when district name plus province identifies one reconciled
district; this covers post-split codes such as Kỳ Anh, Duyên Hải, and Long Mỹ
without conflating reused codes.

The pass shortlisted 6,171 unique QIDs, refreshed them through batched
`wbgetentities`, and produced 6,100 collision-free high-confidence matches.
Incremental refresh reused 6,169 same-day API records and fetched only the two
QIDs introduced by the district-alias correction.

The remaining 3,935 rows drove the saved unrestricted query
`queries/ward-wikidata-predecessor-unresolved.rq`. QLever evaluated 11,853 exact
Vietnamese/ASCII label and alias terms in 5,523 ms, returning 16,666 rows over
10,926 items. Bulk P131 reduction left only 75 QIDs for Action API verification,
from which 54 additional matches were safe.

including every rejected or already-assigned candidate, the reconciled former
district parent, successor QIDs, and the applicable signed 2025 resolution.
`statements/na-wards-create-predecessors.qs` is the single consolidated upload

districts, referenced to 34 official resolution URLs.

The CREATE blocks contain only label, description, P31, P17, and historical
district P131. Dissolution and succession are intentionally deferred to the
lineage batch so they cannot be emitted before the returned QIDs are ingested.
