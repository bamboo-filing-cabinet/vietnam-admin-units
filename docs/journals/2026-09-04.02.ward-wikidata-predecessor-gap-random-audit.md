# Ward predecessor-gap random audit

**Date:** 2026-09-04

## Decision

Do not upload the predecessor CREATE draft. A reproducible simple random sample
of 50 provisional gaps found 14 existing predecessor items, a 28% sample miss
rate. The old exact-name plus terminal-district gate is therefore not adequate
evidence of absence.

The sample used the 50 lowest values of
`SHA-256("2026-09-04-predecessor-gap-audit-v1" + NUL + local_id)` from the
3,879-row baseline manifest. This is a uniform fixed-seed sample without
replacement, recorded as five batches of ten. Live evidence came from broader
`wbsearchentities` searches, batched `wbgetentities`, and Vietnamese Wikipedia
search/pageprops/intro extracts at `2026-09-04T15:36:18Z`.

## Failure modes found

The sample exposed four systematic false-negative paths:

1. **Earlier valid parent.** Mỹ Tân, Bích Đào, Chiềng Đen, Lay Nưa, Mỹ Đức, and
   Quảng Lạc retain P131 values from before a later district transfer or rename.
   Matching only the terminal parent rejects the correct item.
2. **Missing or successor P131.** Lạc Tấn, Trung Đô, An Phú Thuận, and Tráng
   Việt have absent or non-district current P131 data but strong article,
   class, alias, and coordinate identity evidence.
3. **Qualified labels.** Định Bình uses a comma-qualified label; the exact
   literal query did not retrieve it under the unqualified canonical name.
4. **Current/predecessor reversal.** Q10787225 retains the old Luận Thành
   commune's class, district, aliases, and coordinates, while Q135232457 owns
   the article for the larger commune explicitly established in 2025. The
   current mapping had selected the old item. It is corrected to Q135232457,
   releasing Q10787225 for the predecessor.

Two selected canonical items also have likely duplicates: Lạc Tấn
Q32282165 and Chiềng Đen Q32262470 are coordinate-only alternatives to the
article-owning Q10788453 and Q10747291. They were recorded as rejected
duplicates rather than assigned.

## Applied corrections and gate

The 14 reviewed predecessor assignments raise coverage from 6,156 to 6,170 of
10,035. Fifteen reform edges were unlocked, raising edges with both endpoints
from 6,479 to 6,494. The regenerated draft contains 3,865 provisional CREATE
rows.

`data/ward-wikidata-create-predecessors-preflight.json` now includes the sample
result and sets `upload_ready=false` with
`SAMPLED-AUDIT-FAILED 14/50 existing predecessor items`. This failure is
intentional and must remain until the broader reconciliation is repaired and a
fresh audit clears.

## Next action

Extend full-set reconciliation to consider every valid historical parent span,
comma-qualified labels, strong article/coordinate evidence where P131 is
missing, and explicit current/predecessor swaps. Regenerate the provisional set
and repeat a fresh random audit before seeking upload authorization.
