# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 930/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `930/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-05-16` remains
primary-source-open. One supplied source provides strong contemporaneous
state-press corroboration and another confirms the instrument-to-place
identity, but none publishes the complete enacted text or an original official
artifact.

## Contemporaneous establishment announcement

Báo Đầu Tư published an interview with Mai Chí Tính, then Deputy Secretary of
the town Party Committee and Chair of the Giá Rai town People's Committee:

<https://baodautu.vn/phat-trien-gia-rai-bac-lieu--xung-tam-voi-vi-the-d29779.html>

The article says that the newly established Giá Rai town in Bạc Liêu was being
formally announced that day under Resolution 930, dated 2015-05-15, of the
National Assembly Standing Committee. It prints the code as
`930/NQ/UBTVQH 13`; the separators and spacing are editorial typography rather
than a competing formal code. The interview also discusses Giá Rai's urban
planning and refers to the new town government in operation.

Classification: `state_press_secondary`. This is strong contemporaneous
corroboration for the instrument's identity, issuer, issue date, Giá Rai scope,
and implementation. It is not the enacted resolution and does not reproduce
the clauses establishing the three wards.

## TVPL identity reference

TVPL's 2022 explainer listing Vietnam's district-level towns includes Giá Rai
under Bạc Liêu and identifies Resolution 930/NQ-UBTVQH13 of 2015 as its legal
basis:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/ho-tro-phap-luat/tu-van-phap-luat/43113/tieu-chuan-cua-thi-xa-quy-dinh-the-nao-danh-sach-cac-thi-xa-tai-viet-nam>

Classification: `secondary_identity_reference`. The page establishes the
code-place-year relationship but supplies neither the exact date nor any
operative text. It is less probative than a dedicated legal transcription.

## Unrelated 2025 Cà Mau page

The official Tam Giang Tây commune page concerns the 2025 proposal to merge
Bạc Liêu and Cà Mau provinces and to combine Tam Giang Tây with Tân Ân Tây:

<https://tamgiangtay.ngochien.camau.gov.vn/tin-tuc-su-kien/hoi-nghi-cu-tri-thong-qua-de-an-sap-nhap-don-vi-hanh-chinh-120998>

Its legal framework is the 2025 reorganization, including Resolution
76/2025/UBTVQH15 and Government Resolution 74/NQ-CP. The page contains no
reference to Resolution 930 or Giá Rai.

Classification: `official_but_unrelated`. An official host does not make an
artifact provenance for a different instrument, place, and administrative
event.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO instrument key uses `2015-05-16`, while the contemporaneous Báo
Đầu Tư report identifies Resolution 930 as dated `2015-05-15`. The one-day
difference is recorded here. Because no complete enacted text has been found,
this review does not infer an effective-date clause or replace the NSO date.
