# How often does ward composition change?

Written 2026-07-10. Measures the temporal density of ward-level (`Phường`/`Xã`)
change, to size the change-discovery workstream (`.11`, DESIGN §Temporal scope).
Method: national ward snapshots via SOAP `DenNgay` at Jan-1 of each year, diffed
by `MaPhuongXa` code (added/removed) and by name on kept codes (renamed).

## Result: changes nearly every year, in two modes

| interval | wards | added | removed | renamed | churn |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2015→2016 | 11,164 | 3 | 1 | 34 | 38 |
| 2016→2017 | 11,164 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **0** |
| 2017→2018 | 11,164 | 0 | 0 | 7 | 7 |
| 2018→2019 | 11,164 | 0 | 0 | 14 | 14 |
| **2019→2020** | 10,778 | 3 | 389 | 281 | **673** |
| 2020→2021 | 10,615 | 0 | 163 | 152 | 315 |
| 2021→2022 | 10,611 | 0 | 4 | 33 | 37 |
| 2022→2023 | 10,611 | 0 | 0 | 17 | 17 |
| 2023→2024 | 10,610 | 0 | 1 | 46 | 47 |
| **2024→2025** | 10,039 | 0 | 571 | 399 | **970** |
| 2025-01→06 | 10,035 | 3 | 7 | 14 | 24 |

- **Steady trickle:** ~10–50 wards change even in quiet years — mostly renames,
  a few upgrades. Only one interval (2016→2017) had zero.
- **Merger waves:** 2019→2021 (~670 + ~315) and 2024→2025 (~970) are the commune-
  consolidation programs (Resolution **653/2019/UBTVQH14** for 2019–21;
  Resolution **35/2023/UBTVQH15** for 2023–25), hundreds dissolved/renamed each,
  *before* the 2025 province reform.

## Why the true frequency is higher than this table

1. **Annual granularity hides intra-year events.** A wave-year's ~970 changes
   landed across **several distinct Nghị quyết effective dates** within the year
   (each arrangement resolution has its own date; there can be many per year).
   The count of distinct *change dates* > count of *years with changes*.
2. **Code reuse undercounts merges.** When a merged commune keeps one
   constituent's code, the diff sees a rename, not a removal — so "removed"
   understates real merges.
3. **Renames include cosmetic fixes** (diacritics/spelling), which inflate the
   "renamed" column a little — separate signal from territorial change.

## Implications for the model

- **Ward change-discovery must be event-level**, from the **Lịch Sử change-log**
  (decree + exact effective date per unit), not annual snapshots — a yearly
  cadence would smear multiple resolutions into one and miss the exact dates the
  chained lineage + `P585`/`P571`/`P576` qualifiers need.
- **Expect the bulk of all historical events at ward level**, clustered in the
  2019–21 and 2023–25 waves plus the 2025 reform — thousands of edges total.
- Reinforces phasing: provinces (Phase 1) are genuinely low-volume and low-
  cadence; the ward tier (Phase 1b) is where the temporal density — and the
  name→code disambiguation (`.11`) — actually bites.

## Caveat

Diffs computed on `MaPhuongXa` national code + name only; not cross-checked
against Lịch Sử decrees here (that's the Phase-1b ingest). Figures are a lower
bound on event volume, adequate for cadence sizing.

**Exact counts are soft (±~20).** `2026-07-10.14` found the SOAP service's
historical snapshots drift by ~18 records between calls (e.g. 2019-01-01 read
11,164 here, 11,181 later). The cadence *pattern* holds; the precise integers in
the table above should not be treated as exact. Date lineage from Lịch Sử
decrees, not from these diffs.
