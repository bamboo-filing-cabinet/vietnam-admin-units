# Phase 3 ward-crosswalk spike — probe results + go/no-go

Written 2026-07-24 (spike planned 2026-07-20). Executes
`docs/DESIGN-phase3-ward-crosswalk-spike.md` and
`docs/plans/2026-07-20-phase3-ward-crosswalk-spike.md`.

## What / why

Phase 3 is the ward tier (`xã`/`phường`), the largest, highest-churn layer
(~10k units, feeds NA16). Before committing a build order, this spike proves the
one dependency districts never exercised: **does the district-proven Đối Chiếu
crosswalk-sweep method hold at ward scale and volume, and does the yearly-window
blind spot actually bite?**

It also completes a reframe begun in the district recon: **Lịch Sử is an
inventory, not an event source** at sub-province level (district finding
`2026-07-13.01`). The DESIGN "hybrid: Lịch Sử events" assumption is retired here;
the real change-discovery mechanism is the **Đối Chiếu crosswalk yearly-sweep +
Nghị quyết/Nghị định cross-check**.

## Cấp=Xã mechanics

- **Combo value confirmed live: `cmbCap = "3"`** (Tỉnh=1, Huyện=2, Xã=3). The
  `--tier ward` fetch drove the page and returned real data on the first try — no
  recovery probe needed.
- **Export schema: fixed 13-column, province-parented** — identical shape to the
  district export, confirming journal `2026-07-10.06`. Raw header (file order,
  before pandas suffixes the duplicate base/compare names):

  `Tỉnh · Tên Tỉnh · Xã · Tên Xã · Nghị định · Ngày hiệu lực` (base) ·
  `Tên Xã DC · Xã DC · Nghị định · Ngày hiệu lực · Tên Tỉnh DC · Tỉnh DC` (compare) ·
  `Ghi Chú`.

  Spelling is **"DC"** (not the province reader's "ĐC"). Locked into
  `_WARD_HEADER`; the reader's schema guard compares the full header (count +
  order) so any deviating window raises rather than silently mislabeling.
- **The export DROPS the district (Mã QH / Tên QH) columns** shown in the
  on-screen grid. So the pre-2025 ward's district code — the disambiguation key
  DESIGN calls for — is **not in the crosswalk**; it must come from SOAP
  (`DanhMucQuanHuyen`) at build time, sourced separately. (Note: Lịch Sử at Xã
  *does* carry the district columns — see below — but as inventory, not events.)
- Codes are kept verbatim as zero-padded strings (`01117`, `03006`), not Excel
  floats. Effective dates convert cleanly to ISO.
- The 13-column schema is **identical across all five sampled windows** (the
  schema-guarded reader parsed every one without raising) — no window-shape branch
  needed.

## Per-window volume table

Counted by **structural row classification** (the district-proven
`window_events`/`classify_change` model), NOT by `ghi_chu`-non-empty and NOT by
date-filtering `succ_hieu_luc`. A row is an *event* iff its base/successor columns
differ structurally: create (blank base) / dissolve (blank successor) / reparent
(province differs) / rename-or-retype (name differs); a code-only re-code with the
same name+province is `unchanged`. Rows are deduped on the full `_WARD_COLS` tuple
before classification.

| window | raw | dedup | exact_dup_rows | base_distinct | succ_distinct | events | created | dissolved | changed | dated_in_window | prose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2017→2018 (quiet) | 11250 | 11169 | 81 | 11164 | 11164 | **9** | 0 | 0 | 9 | 7 | 6 |
| 2019→2020 (wave) | 11250 | 11171 | 79 | 11164 | 10778 | **674** | 4 | 390 | 280 | 107 | 408 |
| 2024→2025 (wave) | 10637 | 10613 | 24 | 10610 | 10039 | **961** | 0 | 572 | 389 | 168 | 578 |
| 2025-06-30→07-01 (reform) | 10040 | 10040 | 0 | 10035 | **3321** | **9322** | 5 | 6719 | 2598 | 0 | 4359 |
| 2002→2025-06-30 (flat) | 11162 | 11160 | 2 | 10523 | 10035 | CODE-MAP | — | — | — | — | — |

Notes:
- **`events` varies as required** — 9 (quiet) → 674 (2019) → 961 (2024) → 9322
  (reform) — and tracks the `2026-07-10.13` cadence (~670 in 2019–21, ~970 in
  2024–25). The classifier is correctly wired.
- **Reform `succ_distinct` = 3321** is the new-ward count (the 10,039→3,321
  collapse). New wards mostly inherit a predecessor's code via the structured
  `Xã DC` link, so they carry a **non-blank** `base_ma`; only **`created` = 5**
  rows are blank-base "no old counterpart" units (the đặc khu — head rows show Đặc
  khu Bạch Long Vĩ, Cồn Cỏ, Hoàng Sa). Keep the two metrics separate.
- The flat 2002→2025 file is a **CODE-MAP**, not events: 10,522 rows are
  `"Chuyển đổi mã"` boilerplate. It crosses the 2004 renumber, so `classify()`
  would count the whole-file remap as change — meaningless. Kept only as the ward
  code-remap table.
- **Ward code widths (from the flat file): old = 7-digit, current = 5-digit
  `MaPhuongXa`** — i.e. the ward remap is **7→5**, NOT the district-style 5→3
  (base_ma width dist: 7-digit ×10524; succ_ma: 5-digit ×10037). This corrects the
  assumption that the district recode shape carries over.

## `Ngày hiệu lực` / `Ghi Chú` behavior

- **Dates populate** and convert to ISO. `dated_in_window` (events whose
  `succ_hieu_luc` falls in `[base, compare)`) is a *sanity signal*, not the count:
  it runs lower than `events` (e.g. 107/674 in 2019) because many event dates live
  in `base_hieu_luc` or are blank — exactly the district caveat. **Never
  substitute `dated_in_window` for `events`.**
- **Reform `dated_in_window` = 0 is a boundary artifact**, not a problem: the
  reform effective date is `2025-07-01`, which equals the `compare` bound, and the
  filter is `< compare`. The dates are present (`succ_hieu_luc` = 2025-07-01 on the
  compare side); they just sit on the exclusive edge.
- **Composition prose is present per-event in single-code-era windows** (`prose` =
  6 / 408 / 578 / 4359), and is pure `"Chuyển đổi mã"` boilerplate in the flat
  cross-era file — matching the district case.

## Blind-spot verdict — the crosswalk is net-only

Net-only is a **structural** property of a two-snapshot diff (a diff of two Jan-1
states cannot contain an intermediate state). The probe **confirms empirically on
all four sampled event windows** that the export behaves as a pure 2-point diff:
grouping by the province-qualified `(base_tinh, base_ma)` key, **0 base wards
carry >1 distinct successor effective date in any window**.

Distinct in-window effective dates observed (the waves step across many Nghị quyết
dates — the cross-check the crosswalk cannot reconcile by itself):

- 2017: `2017-04-19, 2017-08-18, 2017-12-13` (3 dates)
- 2019: 12 dates (`2019-01-10 … 2019-12-01`)
- 2024: `2024-02-01, 2024-05-01, 2024-09-01, 2024-11-01, 2024-12-01` (5 dates)
- 2025 reform: none in-window (all dated on the `2025-07-01` compare edge)

**Consequence (stands regardless):** the blind spot is real and **cannot be
detected or sized from the crosswalk alone** — a ward changing twice within one
window year, or created-and-dissolved between the Jan-1 endpoints, is invisible
here. So even the `events` column above is a **lower bound**.

**Sub-finding (build-time input):** sizing the missed in-year events needs the
per-event **Nghị quyết UBTVQH** list (e.g. `653/2019/UBTVQH14` for 2019–21,
`35/2023/UBTVQH15` for 2023–25). Sampled `Ghi Chú` in the ward windows cites Nghị
quyết codes like `Số: 767/NQ-UBTVQH14` and `Số: 1669/NQ-UBTVQH15` — a **different
source** from the `NghiDinh.aspx` decree list `crosscheck_decrees.py` scraped for
districts (544 recs). **Do NOT assume `NghiDinh.aspx` covers ward waves.** Locating
the correct GSO page for ward-arrangement Nghị quyết is itself unresolved and
becomes the **first build-time task**; it is out of spike scope.

## Lịch Sử at Cấp=Xã — still an inventory

Drove `Lich_Su_Moi.aspx` at Cấp=Xã (`cmbCap="3"`) and inspected the rendered
`grid0` schema. Columns:

`Mã · Tên · Tên Tiếng Anh · Cấp · Nghị định · Mã QH · Quận/Huyện · Mã TP · Tỉnh/Thành Phố`

These are **current-state inventory attributes** (code, name, English name, level,
defining decree, parent district code+name, parent province code+name) — there are
**no old→new "ĐC" change columns**. It is a point-in-time hierarchical inventory,
NOT an event/change timeline — confirming the district finding `2026-07-13.01` at
ward level. (It does carry the `Mã QH`/district columns the crosswalk drops, but
as inventory, so it is not a substitute for the crosswalk's lineage.) The stale
"hybrid: Lịch Sử events" assumption is retired (see `DESIGN.md` decision 4 +
`DESIGN-phase3.md` note).

## GO / NO-GO

**GO for the Đối Chiếu crosswalk as the net per-window lineage backbone for wards
— pending a Nghị quyết-list magnitude check.**

The method transfers cleanly to ward scale: the combo value works, the schema is
the district schema, the reader parses all windows under a strict schema guard,
effective dates and composition prose are present per-event in single-era windows,
and the structural event counts vary and track the known cadence. This is a real
GO for the lineage backbone.

It is **not** an unconditional GO: the crosswalk is net-only, so the yearly sweep
misses same-unit-twice-in-year and ephemeral within-year units, and that gap is
**not sizeable from the crosswalk itself**. Bounding it requires the per-event Nghị
quyết UBTVQH list, whose source page is unresolved. That magnitude check is the
first build-time task, before finalizing lineage.

## Recommended full-window list for the ward build

- **Yearly EVENT windows** `01/01/Y → 01/01/(Y+1)` for **Y = 2005 … 2024** (20
  windows), each within the post-2004 5-digit code-era. Start at **2005→2006**, not
  2004→2005: per the district precedent (`district_model.py` excludes 2004→2005 as
  a recode/alias source, not events).
- **`2004→2005` recode boundary** — CODE-MAP/recode, not an event window (the
  post-code-change alias source).
- **Flat `2002→2025-06-30` code map** — CODE-MAP/recode; the ward code-remap table
  (old **7-digit** → current **5-digit** `MaPhuongXa`).
- **`2025-06-30 → 2025-07-01` reform boundary** — the 10,039→3,321 collapse + đặc
  khu / new-ward creations; a clean reform-day window (not a current-date pull,
  which would mix in post-reform churn).

Note: pre-2004 ward *events* are below the crosswalk floor (hard event floor at the
2004 code change, `2026-07-13.02`) — a later, non-GSO concern.

## Recommended build order — chronological 2005→forward

**Chronological `2005→forward` for the lineage backbone**, not anchor-2025-back.
One-line reason: the 2025 reform is by far the largest window (9,322 events) and
its new wards attach to predecessors via the structured `Xã DC` link, so building
the pre-2025 history first gives the reform well-formed predecessors to point at —
chronological forward keeps lineage parent-before-child and avoids dangling
backward references. (Caveat: the 2025 reform slice is self-contained and is the
highest-value deliverable for NA16, so it can be reconciled to Wikidata as an
independent anchor slice first if NA16 delivery is time-pressured — but the
backbone build order is forward.)

## New hazards surfaced

- **Exact-duplicate FULL rows in the crosswalk export itself** — 81 (2017), 79
  (2019), 24 (2024), 2 (flat), 0 (reform). This is a *different* measure/source
  from the SOAP-snapshot duplicates in `2026-07-10.14` (17 in 2019, 19 in 2020),
  and it is larger. The ingest **must dedupe on the full `_WARD_COLS` tuple** per
  window before classifying (mostly landing on unchanged rows, but the dedupe is
  mandatory so a duplicate on a changed row cannot inflate counts).
- **Ward code width is 7→5, not 5→3** — the flat file's old codes are 7-digit; do
  not carry over the district recode shape into ward `local_id`/recode logic.
- **đặc-khu created count = 5** at the reform in this window (blank-base "no old
  counterpart"); watch for the known đặc-khu 12-vs-13 wobble (`DESIGN.md`) when the
  full reform slice is built.
- **The crosswalk's own decree column is per the district finding unreliable**
  (cumulative "last-touching" decree, not the in-window event decree — e.g. a
  2019→2020 row citing a `2025` Nghị quyết). Source decree/Nghị quyết numbers by
  unit + date from the Nghị quyết list / `Ghi Chú`, not the crosswalk's decree
  column.
- **Nghị quyết source page unresolved** — ward waves are Nghị quyết UBTVQH, likely
  NOT on `NghiDinh.aspx`; finding the right GSO page is the first build task.

## Follow-up (2026-07-24): Nghị quyết source resolved — it's `NghiDinh.aspx`

The spike flagged "locate the Nghị quyết UBTVQH list page" as the first build task
and warned *"do NOT assume `NghiDinh.aspx` covers ward waves."* A quick probe
settles it: **`NghiDinh.aspx` DOES cover them.** It is a **mixed legal-document
list**, not Nghị-định-only.

- The 544-row main table contains **202 UBTVQH Nghị quyết**, of which **177
  explicitly mention ward-level arrangement** ("sắp xếp đơn vị hành chính cấp xã của
  tỉnh …"), each with a per-resolution effective date.
- The exact codes the ward crosswalk cited are present: `767/NQ-UBTVQH14`,
  `1669/NQ-UBTVQH15`, `1673/NQ-UBTVQH15`.
- UBTVQH rows by effective year track the known waves: 2015=11, 2017=3, 2018=7,
  2019=13, **2020=49, 2021=14**, 2022=4, 2023=10, **2024=35, 2025=55**, 2026=1.
- Per-resolution effective dates collapse onto a handful of distinct dates per wave
  year — and **the 2024 dates match the crosswalk's surfaced dates exactly**,
  independently confirming the two sources agree:

  | year | UBTVQH resolutions | distinct effective dates |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 2020 | 49 | 5 (`01-01, 02-01, 03-01, 06-01, 11-01`) |
  | 2024 | 35 | 5 (`02-01, 05-01, 09-01, 11-01, 12-01`) |
  | 2025 | 55 | 4 (`01-01, 02-01, 03-01, 07-01`) |

**Consequences for the build:**
- **No new scraper.** The existing `crosscheck_decrees.py` already keys on
  `NQ|NĐ|QH` and grabs the 544-row table; extend its `_STRUCT`/classifier to
  ward-level Nghị quyết (currently it excludes ward ops nested `… thuộc huyện`).
- **Window strategy = event-driven, keyed to these effective dates.** ~4–5 windows
  per wave year (day-before → day-of each distinct date), not a monthly walk. This
  eliminates the same-unit-twice-in-year blind spot (a unit can't change twice on
  one effective date) at a fraction of a monthly sweep's cost.
- **The criteria-framework resolutions** `653/2019/UBTVQH14` and `35/2023/UBTVQH15`
  are **NOT** in the list (they set arrangement criteria, not per-unit events) — the
  per-province arrangement resolutions we need for the cross-check are.

This updates the GO verdict from "GO-pending-magnitude, source unresolved" to
**GO — the magnitude cross-check source is in hand (`NghiDinh.aspx`); the remaining
build-time work is extending `crosscheck_decrees.py` to wards and running the
event-driven sweep.**
