# Ward legal-index correction: 14/2008/NĐ-CP versus 14/2008/QH12

Date: 2026-08-31

The supplied sources resolve both context-only `14/2008/NĐ-CP` rows in the NSO
legal index. They do not provide provenance for the Tân Đức transfer. Instead,
they prove that the two rows combine the code of an unrelated Government
decree with the title and observation dates of Resolution 14/2008/QH12.

## Actual Decree 14/2008/NĐ-CP

The official Government page identifies Decree 14/2008/NĐ-CP as issued on
2008-02-04 and titled:

> Quy định tổ chức các cơ quan chuyên môn thuộc Ủy ban nhân dân huyện, quận,
> thị xã, thành phố thuộc tỉnh

<https://chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?pageid=27160&docid=57075>

The page exposes the original Word attachment:

<https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2008/02/59034_nd14cp.doc>

Offline extraction verifies a nine-page decree with 14 articles. It regulates
the functions and organization of specialized agencies under district-level
People's Committees. Article 13 makes it effective 15 days after Gazette
publication and replaces Decree 172/2004/NĐ-CP. It contains no Tân Đức,
Hà Tây, Phú Thọ, Việt Trì, or administrative-boundary transfer clause.

The official VBPL properties record gives the exact legal dates: issued
2008-02-04, published in Gazette issues 103–104 on 2008-02-10, and effective
2008-02-25:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=12850&Keyword=>

The supplied TVPL and LuatVietnam pages independently reproduce the same code,
date, title, subject, and operative text. They are retained as secondary checks,

| `14/2008/NĐ-CP@2008-08-03` | the same title repeated at the next SOAP observation |

Neither combination is a real enactment. The official decree predates both by
months and concerns administrative organization, while the two raw titles
describe a province-boundary transfer. An official source matching each raw
code-title-date combination therefore cannot exist.

The raw index rows remain unchanged. Their derived records retain
`classification: duplicate_or_superseded` and
`observation_status: superseded_by_canonical_correction`, and now carry
`source_status: invalid_index_identity_verified`. This status closes an
impossible index identity without falsely attaching the unrelated actual
decree as the source of the Tân Đức transfer.

## Canonical Tân Đức authority

The canonical instrument is National Assembly Resolution 14/2008/QH12, issued
2008-05-29 and effective 2008-07-01:

> Điều chỉnh địa giới hành chính giữa tỉnh Hà Tây và tỉnh Phú Thọ, giữa tỉnh
> Bình Phước và tỉnh Đồng Nai

Its already archived official provincial-government copy is:

`legal/ward/2008-07-01/14-2008-qh12.supplemental.doc`

Article 1.1(a) transfers all 454.08 ha and 2,721 people of Xã Tân Đức from
huyện Ba Vì, Hà Tây to thành phố Việt Trì, Phú Thọ. Article 4 sets the
2008-07-01 effective date. The SOAP interval
`soap:2008-07-02->2008-08-03` observes the delayed same-code parent/province
change for code `09622`; Resolution 14/2008/QH12 remains linked to that
component. Resolution 15/2008/QH12 separately governs the other Hà Tây/Hà Nội
changes in the same interval.
