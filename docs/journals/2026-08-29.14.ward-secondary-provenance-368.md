# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `368/NQ-UBTVQH14@2017-04-19` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish unusually strong official
downstream provenance for the resolution's exact identity, date, full subject,
implementation, and conversion of Sầm Sơn town into a provincial city. None is
the complete enacted resolution.

## Ministry of Justice implementation appendix

LuatVietnam publishes a complete transcription of Ministry of Justice Letter
1183/BTP-CNTT, dated 2020-03-31:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/tu-phap/cong-van-1183-btp-cntt-2020-cap-nhat-ten-don-vi-hanh-chinh-tren-he-thong-thong-tin-dang-ky-311906-d6.html>

The Ministry of Justice also publishes the letter on its official portal and
lists original DOC and PDF attachments:

<https://www.moj.gov.vn/qt/tintuc/Pages/chi-dao-dieu-hanh.aspx?ItemID=2852>

Appendix 01 identifies Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14, its 2017-04-19 issuance
date, Thanh Hóa province, and the complete subject: establishing Quảng Cư,
Quảng Châu, Quảng Thọ, and Quảng Vinh wards in Sầm Sơn town and establishing
Sầm Sơn city. It records the resolution as `Đã đồng bộ` in the civil-status
registration and management information system.

Classification: `official_downstream_implementation_original`, with the
LuatVietnam page serving as a `secondary_official_document_transcription`.
The ministry's original letter and appendix are strong official evidence that
the administrative changes were implemented, but they reproduce an identity
and synchronization table rather than the target's enacted clauses.

## Official National Assembly follow-on proceeding

The National Assembly portal reports the 2017-08-11 Standing Committee
proceeding to establish the People's Court and People's Procuracy of Sầm Sơn
city:

<https://quochoi.vn/_catalogs/masterpage/TVPortal.Publishing.Subsite.aspx?ItemID=33732>

The procuracy submission correctly identifies Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14,
dated 2017-04-19, as establishing Sầm Sơn city on the basis of Sầm Sơn town.
The proposed city procuracy would inherit the town procuracy's staff and
organization. The page therefore supplies official institutional evidence that
the city transition had taken legal and practical effect by August 2017.

The page contains an internal transcription error in its preceding court
paragraph: it calls the instrument `386/NQ-UBTVQH14` and lists only Quảng Châu,
Quảng Thọ, and Quảng Vinh, omitting Quảng Cư. The correct `368` appears in the
procuracy paragraph and is independently confirmed by the Ministry of Justice,
NSO register, and every other supplied source. The error is retained here and
must not be treated as an alternate instrument identity.

Classification: `official_downstream_parliamentary_press`. This is the
strongest supplied official contemporary narrative, but its subject is the
later court and procuracy resolution rather than the enacted administrative
resolution.

## National Assembly newspaper account

Báo Đại biểu Nhân dân, whose governing body is the Office of the National
Assembly, reports the same 2017-08-11 proceeding:

<https://daibieunhandan.vn/thong-qua-nghi-quyet-ve-viec-thanh-lap-tand-vksnd-tp-sam-son-thanh-hoa-10207193.html>

It correctly identifies Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14 and 2017-04-19, says Sầm
Sơn city was established on the basis of Sầm Sơn town, and reports that the
city's People's Council, People's Committee, and other institutions were
already operating. It does not repeat the National Assembly portal's `386`
error.

Classification: `official_parliamentary_press_downstream`. It is useful
official corroboration, not a copy of Resolution 368.

VietnamPlus and Báo Tin Tức publish substantially the same Vietnam News Agency
report about the court and procuracy proceeding:

- <https://www.vietnamplus.vn/thong-qua-nghi-quyet-ve-thanh-lap-toa-an-nhan-dan-thanh-pho-sam-son-post460610.vnp>
- <https://baotintuc.vn/chinh-tri/ubtv-quoc-hoi-thong-qua-nghi-quyet-thanh-lap-tand-vksnd-tp-sam-son-thanh-hoa-20170811164032691.htm>

Both correctly identify Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14, the 2017-04-19 date, and
the establishment of Sầm Sơn city from Sầm Sơn town. They also say the city's
People's Council, People's Committee, and other bodies were already operating.

Classification: `state_news_downstream_syndication`. These are useful
contemporaneous state-news corroboration, but they are not two independent
evidentiary accounts: both carry the same TTXVN reporting and concern the later
judicial institutions.

## Thanh Hóa retrospective

Báo Thanh Hóa's 2023 retrospective describes the administrative development
of the coastal city:

<https://baothanhhoa.vn/do-thi-bien-sam-son-mot-hanh-trinh-phat-trien-184144.htm>

The rendered article explicitly identifies Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14 and
2017-04-19 and says Sầm Sơn was recognized as a provincial city. It reports
the later city as having 11 commune-level units and nearly 110,000 residents,
but does not list those units or attribute those current figures to the
resolution's operative provisions.

Classification: `official_local_press_retrospective`. It is valuable local
corroboration for the legal milestone and resulting city, not clause-level
evidence for the four ward conversions.

## VTV retrospective

VTV's 2024 account of Sầm Sơn's development likewise identifies Resolution

a provincial city:

<https://vtv.vn/xa-hoi/hanh-trinh-di-len-cua-mot-do-thi-bien-sam-son-20240802220932855.htm>

Classification: `state_media_retrospective`. It independently corroborates
the correct instrument identity and city transition, but adds no ward-level
operative detail.

## Coverage and remaining gaps

Taken together, the Ministry of Justice appendix supplies the exact full title
and confirms downstream database implementation. The National Assembly and
National Assembly newspaper reports establish that the new city's political,
court, and procuracy institutions succeeded those of Sầm Sơn town. State and
local media repeatedly corroborate the correct code, date, and city status.

No supplied source contains the complete target resolution, its recitals, the
operative area and population figures for Quảng Cư, Quảng Châu, Quảng Thọ, and
Quảng Vinh, the resulting area and population of Sầm Sơn city, implementation
clauses, signature block, or a complete effective-date provision. The
National Assembly page's isolated `386` typo also makes it unsuitable as a
standalone identity source.

The NSO register records both issuance and observation/effect on 2017-04-19,
so this review exposes no date discrepancy comparable to the 2015 instruments.
