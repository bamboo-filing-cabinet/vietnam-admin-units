# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 891/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

`891/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12` remains primary-source-open, but the supplied
sources now establish complete secondary text provenance plus independent
official-secondary corroboration.

## Complete LuatVietnam transcription

LuatVietnam publishes the complete enacted text of Resolution
891/NQ-UBTVQH13:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/dia-gioi-hanh-chinh/nghi-quyet-891-nq-ubtvqh13-2015-thanh-lap-thi-xa-dong-trieu-tinh-quang-ninh-272471-d1.html>

The transcription contains the formal header and issue date of 2015-03-11,
resolution title, recitals, all operative clauses, implementation clause,
effective-date clause, distribution list, and signature block for National
Assembly Chair Nguyễn Sinh Hùng.

Article 1 establishes Đông Triều town from the entire former Đông Triều
district: 39,721.55 hectares, 173,141 residents, and 21 commune-level units. It
also establishes these six wards:

- Đông Triều from Đông Triều township;
- Mạo Khê from Mạo Khê township;
- Đức Chính from Đức Chính commune;
- Hưng Đạo from Hưng Đạo commune;
- Xuân Sơn from Xuân Sơn commune; and
- Kim Sơn from Kim Sơn commune.

Article 3 states that the resolution takes effect from the date of signing, so
the transcribed issue and effective date is 2015-03-11.

Classification: `secondary_full_text`. The text is complete enough to support
the resolution's identity, clauses, date, and signatory, but it remains a
commercially hosted transcription rather than an official original artifact.

## Official Quảng Ninh corroboration

The Quảng Ninh provincial government history page independently records the
same code, date, and town-level operation:

<https://www.quangninh.gov.vn/Trang/ChiTietBVGioiThieu.aspx?bvid=544>

It states that on 2015-03-11 the Standing Committee of the National Assembly
issued Resolution 891/NQ-UBTVQH13, establishing Đông Triều town from the entire
area and population of Đông Triều district.

Classification: `official_secondary`. This first-party provincial history is
strong corroboration, but it provides only a summary sentence rather than the
complete enacted text or original file.

## Registry treatment

The pinned NSO key remains `891/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-03-12`. The complete
transcription and official provincial history instead place both issue and
effectiveness on 2015-03-11, one day before the NSO date. That discrepancy is
retained here rather than normalized away.

No source-status or coverage count changes result from this review. The TVPL
URL was already counted as secondary evidence; the stronger LuatVietnam full
text and Quảng Ninh official-secondary page are preserved in this journal.
Primary closure still requires a complete enacted text or original PDF/DOC
from an official National Assembly, Government, VBPL, Gazette, or Quảng Ninh
government host.
