## Official identity and legal dates

The official VBPL record is Item 12656:

- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=12656>
- <https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=12656&Keyword=>

Its indexed properties and rendered full text identify Decree 08/NĐ-CP,
signed by Prime Minister Nguyễn Tấn Dũng, concerning the creation of thị xã
Hồng Ngự and its wards. The four relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2008-12-23`;
- Gazette publication date: `2009-01-03`, issues 05 and 06;
- official effective date: `2009-01-18`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2009-01-07`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
NSO date therefore remains an observation key and is not silently rewritten
as the legal effective date.

The official record advertises original attachment `08.NĐ.CP.zip`. That exact
filename is registered as a recovery lead; no attachment URL is asserted
without a retrievable page exposing its href.

## Enacted operations and observed topology

The complete TVPL transcription is:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bat-dong-san/Nghi-dinh-08-ND-CP-de-nghi-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-huyen-Hong-Ngu-thanh-lap-phuong-thuoc-tinh-Dong-Thap-84046.aspx>

Article 1 creates thị xã Hồng Ngự from 12,216.16 hectares and 74,569 people of
huyện Hồng Ngự, comprising all of thị trấn Hồng Ngự and the communes Tân Hội,
Bình Thạnh, An Bình A, and An Bình B, plus 751.72 hectares and 8,380 people
transferred from Xã Thường Lạc.

It establishes three wards:

- An Lộc, from 400.14 hectares and 14,534 people of thị trấn Hồng Ngự;
- An Thạnh, from the remaining 470.10 hectares and 17,074 people of the former
  town; and
- An Lạc, from the transferred 751.72-hectare, 8,380-person portion of Xã
  Thường Lạc.

The new thị xã has seven commune-level units: the three wards and the four
continuing communes Tân Hội, Bình Thạnh, An Bình A, and An Bình B. Xã Thường
Lạc remains in huyện Hồng Ngự with 849.70 hectares and 3,280 people; the
residual district has 11 communes.

The SOAP interval `soap:2009-01-01->2009-01-07` contains five candidate legal
instruments and 83 components. Crosswalk citations and named-unit matching
assign seven components to Decree 08: three `lineage` components for the new
wards and four `parent_or_boundary_only` components for the continuing
communes re-parented into the new town. The partial Thường Lạc transfer is
represented through the An Lạc lineage while the surviving commune retains
its identity. The topology agrees with the complete text but cannot substitute
for archived official bytes.

## Contemporaneous Government implementation decision

The supplied Government portal page is not a copy of Decree 08. It is Đồng
Tháp Decision 50/QĐ-UBND.HC, issued on 2009-01-09, on directing the province's
2009 socioeconomic and state-budget plan:

<https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/default.aspx?pageid=27160&docid=89531>

Within that official decision, the provincial government directs the
Department of Home Affairs to submit during February 2009 a plan and roadmap
for implementing Decree 08/NĐ-CP, dated 2008-12-23, on adjusting huyện Hồng
Ngự to establish thị xã Hồng Ngự. The Government page exposes the decision's
original Word attachment, `92712_qd50ub.doc`.

Classification: `official_local_contemporaneous_implementation`. This is
strong official corroboration for the decree's code, issue date, territory,
and immediate implementation. It does not reproduce the decree's enacted
clauses and therefore cannot close the primary-source gate.

## Later Ministry classification

LuatVietnam publishes a complete transcription of Ministry of Home Affairs
Decision 293/QĐ-BNV, issued on 2010-04-05:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/hanh-chinh/quyet-dinh-293-qd-bnv-cua-bo-noi-vu-phan-loai-va-dieu-chinh-phan-loai-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-huyen-doi-voi-thi-xa-hong-ngu-va-huyen-hong-ngu-tinh-dong-thap-346082-d1.html>

Its recitals cite Decree 08/NĐ-CP, its 2008-12-23 date, and the Hồng Ngự town
and ward establishments. Article 1 classifies thị xã Hồng Ngự as a type-III
district-level administrative unit and changes huyện Hồng Ngự from type I to
type II. It also cites the province's 2009-03-12 classification submission.

Classification: `secondary_downstream_ministry_decision_transcription`. It
confirms the decree's identity and the durable legal distinction between the
new town and residual district, but it is a later classification act rather
than primary evidence for the 2008 boundary and ward clauses.

## Same-code source disambiguation

A separate Decree 08/NĐ-CP was issued on 2009-02-09 for commune and district
changes in Bến Tre and is already represented by
`08/NĐ-CP@2009-02-24`, with archived Government metadata and its original Word
attachment. The older code-only secondary mapping incorrectly attached the
Hồng Ngự TVPL URL to that later Bến Tre record.

Date-qualified secondary keys and an explicit empty override now assign the
Hồng Ngự URL only to `08/NĐ-CP@2009-01-07`. The incorrect URL was removed from
the Bến Tre registry row and both of its raw-manifest entries.

## Official-source recovery boundary

Direct requests to the VBPL full-text page and likely attachment paths
returned HTTP 403 browser challenges. The legacy Ministry of Justice hostname
did not resolve, and no interactive browser session was available. The
Government legal portal returned no target for an exact Hồng Ngự title search;
its code search returned only substring references to other instruments. A
Government Gazette API title search found no publication PDF.

Search-indexed official output is sufficient to register the exact identity,
dates, complete-text page, and attachment filename as recovery leads, but it
is not a preserved raw artifact. Primary closure still requires saving the
complete official page and `08.NĐ.CP.zip`, recording their provenance and
hashes, and verifying the identity and operative text offline.
