# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Resolution 469/NQ-UBTVQH15

Date: 2026-08-30

The pinned NSO instrument `469/NQ-UBTVQH15@2022-04-10` now has an exact
official document-registry page and PDF lead from Thái Nguyên Social Insurance,

## Established identity and topology

Complete commercial transcriptions identify Resolution 469/NQ-UBTVQH15 as
adopted by the National Assembly Standing Committee on 2022-02-15 and effective
on 2022-04-10. Article 1 converts nine entire communes into same-named wards:

| Former commune | Resulting ward | Area (km²) | Population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Đắc Sơn | Đắc Sơn | 14.36 | 11,198 |
| Đông Cao | Đông Cao | 6.47 | 9,120 |
| Hồng Tiến | Hồng Tiến | 18.47 | 15,076 |
| Nam Tiến | Nam Tiến | 8.31 | 9,124 |
| Tân Hương | Tân Hương | 9.32 | 10,538 |
| Tân Phú | Tân Phú | 4.78 | 7,025 |
| Thuận Thành | Thuận Thành | 5.48 | 9,684 |
| Tiên Phong | Tiên Phong | 14.82 | 16,694 |
| Trung Thành | Trung Thành | 9.09 | 13,151 |

The same article establishes Phổ Yên city from the full 258.42 km² and 231,363
residents of Phổ Yên town. The resulting city has 18 commune-level units: 13
wards and five communes. Thái Nguyên province consequently has nine
district-level units and 178 commune-level units: 128 communes, 41 wards, and
nine townships.

Article 2 establishes the city People's Court and People's Procuracy by
succession from the town institutions. Those institutional provisions are not
ward-lineage events. Article 3 supplies the 2022-04-10 effective date, which
matches the NSO instrument key and observed event; there is no date discrepancy
to preserve.
