# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 39/2006/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `39/2006/NĐ-CP@2006-05-06` now has one complete
commercial legal-database transcription, a near-complete parallel
transcription with material paragraph and clause omissions, an indexed
official VBPL record, official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization metadata, and
contemporaneous Government press corroboration. It remains
primary-source-open because no complete official page or original attachment
could be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The reviewed sources identify Decree 39/2006/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2006-04-21, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc điều chỉnh địa giới hành chính, thành lập các xã thuộc các huyện Chư
> Sê, Kông Chro, Đak Đoa, Ia Grai và KBang, tỉnh Gia Lai

The three relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-04-21`;
- official effective date: `2006-05-15`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-05-06`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL index and Ministry of Home Affairs systematization give
2006-05-15 as the legal effective date. The NSO snapshot observes the seven
new commune codes nine days earlier. Its date remains an observation key and
is not silently rewritten.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 establishes seven communes by transferring territory and population
from eight predecessor communes:

| District | Successor | Code | Predecessor contribution | Successor total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chư Sê | Ia BLứ | `23987` | Ia Le: 19,114.50 ha; 4,688 people | 19,114.50 ha; 4,688 people |
| Kông Chro | Đăk Pơ Pho | `23846` | Yang Trung: 5,305 ha; 2,054 people | 5,305 ha; 2,054 people |
| Kông Chro | Đắk Kơ Ning | `23840` | Yang Nam: 3,553 ha; 740 people; SRó: 9,869.20 ha; 1,503 people | 13,422.20 ha; 2,243 people |
| Đak Đoa | HNol | `23714` | Trang: 4,685 ha; 2,211 people | 4,685 ha; 2,211 people |
| Ia Grai | Ia Bă | `23771` | Ia Hrung: 11,212.05 ha; 5,905 people | 11,212.05 ha; 5,905 people |
| Ia Grai | Ia Yok | `23768` | Ia Sao: 2,642.71 ha; 8,246 people | 2,642.71 ha; 8,246 people |
| KBang | Đak SMar | `23660` | Đông: 12,678.66 ha; 2,012 people | 12,678.66 ha; 2,012 people |

The decree also performs two transfers inside KBang district before recording
the final post-adjustment totals:

- 327 ha of Đông commune passes to KBang township; and
- 187.50 ha and 429 people pass from Krong commune to Đông commune.

The seven creations are partial-territory lineage operations, not whole-unit
retypes. Đắk Kơ Ning has two predecessor contributions; each of the other six
new communes has one. The two KBang transfers alter retained units without
creating an additional roster code.

The Gia Lai portion of `soap:2006-04-15->2006-05-06` agrees exactly on the
seven new codes and their five district parents. Crosswalk rows 25–28 and
31–33 of `crosswalk/ward_2006-01-01_2007-01-01.xls` independently support the
seven successor additions. The same SOAP interval also contains the creation
of Nhồi township and the Quỳnh Thiện-to-Hoàng Mai same-code change; those
components are linked to Decrees 40 and 41, not Decree 39. Snapshot comparison
cannot observe the two KBang boundary transfers because Đông, Krong, and KBang
township all retain their codes.

## Official-source recovery boundary

The official VBPL record is indexed at:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=16253>

The Ministry of Home Affairs' official 2024 systematization volume
independently lists the same code, title, 2006-04-21 issue date, and 2006-05-15
effective date:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/HHTDanhMuc/Attachments/633/Tap%20HTH%202024.pdf>

These official indexed representations establish identity and dates, but they
do not satisfy the raw-artifact gate. Direct requests to the full-text, print,
Ministry of Home Affairs mirror, and properties endpoints returned HTTP 403
challenge pages. The indexed output did not expose an exact attachment
filename, so no filename is guessed or registered. The legacy
`vbpl.moj.gov.vn` hostname failed DNS resolution.

Fresh exact-code and title searches on the Government legal portal found no
matching Decree 39 record; the code search returned only an unrelated Decree
139 substring match. A year-bounded Government Gazette API search returned no
original publication PDF.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with raw retrieval blocked. Primary
closure still requires saving the complete official page or an attachment it
actually exposes, recording its source metadata and hash, and verifying its
identity and contents offline.

## Commercial legal-database records

LuatVietnam publishes a complete transcription:

<https://luatvietnam.vn/chinh-sach/nghi-dinh-39-2006-nd-cp-chinh-phu-29368-d1.html>

It includes the formal identity, recitals, all seven commune creations, both
KBang transfers, predecessor contributions, post-adjustment totals, boundary
descriptions, effectiveness and implementation clauses, and signature block.
Its summary explicitly gives the 2006-05-15 effective date. Classification:
`secondary_full_text`.

Thư Viện Pháp Luật publishes a parallel transcription:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-dinh-39-2006-ND-CP-dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-thanh-lap-xa-thuoc-huyen-Chu-Se-Kong-Chro-Dak-Doa-Ia-Grai-KBang-tinh-Gia-Lai-11592.aspx>

It agrees on the formal identity, all seven creations, their predecessor
contributions, most metrics and boundaries, retained-unit totals, and
operative clauses. However, it omits Đăk Kơ Ning's 13,422.20 ha/2,243-person
successor total and boundary paragraph. It also omits the 187.50 ha and
429-person Krong-to-Đông transfer and consequently numbers the Đak SMar
creation as item 8 instead of item 9. Classification:
`secondary_near_complete_text_with_material_omissions`.

The omissions do not change the seven SOAP roster additions, but they are
material to later boundary lineage. They are preserved rather than silently
repaired in the TVPL transcription; the complete official bytes remain the
preferred adjudication source.

## Government press corroboration

Báo Điện tử Chính phủ published a contemporaneous report on 2006-04-23:

<https://baochinhphu.vn/dieu-chinh-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-cac-xa-thuoc-cac-huyen-chu-se-kong-chro-dak-doa-ia-grai-va-kbang-tinh-gia-lai-10212579.htm>

The report states that Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải signed the decree on
2006-04-21. It identifies all seven new communes, reproduces their areas,
populations, and most boundaries, and independently records both KBang
transfers, including the clause omitted by TVPL. Classification:
`official_government_press_contemporaneous_enactment_corroboration`.

The press report shortens `Đăk Pơ Pho` to `Đăk Pơ`. Both legal-database
transcriptions and the SOAP roster use the full name, so the shorter press
rendering is treated as a news abbreviation or typo rather than a separate
unit. The report is an official contemporaneous summary, not the complete
enacted instrument, and therefore does not close primary provenance.
