# Ward secondary provenance: Bình Phước and Tiền Giang

Date: 2026-08-29

This journal records secondary evidence found while the corresponding official
primary artifacts remain unavailable. These sources improve identity, date,
and operative-change provenance, but they do not close the official-source
gate described in the ward source audit.

## `14/NĐ-CP@2009-04-11`

The pinned NSO legal index places this instrument at 2009-04-11 and describes
the creation of communes in Chơn Thành and Bù Đăng districts, Bình Phước. No
complete enacted text or original file has been recovered from an official
host.

Secondary full-text evidence is available through the Ministry of Home Affairs
letter `2800/BNV-TL`, reproduced by TVPL:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/cong-van/Lao-dong-Tien-luong/Cong-van-2800-BNV-TL-phu-cap-khu-vuc-94174.aspx>

The letter is dated 2009-08-28 and directly states that two Bình Phước communes
were newly established under Decree 14/NĐ-CP dated 2009-04-10. It names xã
Quang Minh, huyện Chơn Thành and xã Đường 10, huyện Bù Đăng. This supports the
instrument identity, the two created communes, and an official issue date one
day before the NSO index date. It is not a reproduction of Decree 14 itself.

LuatVietnam advertises a red-stamped PDF of the same Ministry letter and
exposes a preview endpoint:

- <https://luatvietnam.vn/lao-dong/cong-van-2800-bnv-tl-bo-noi-vu-45437-d6.html>
- <https://luatvietnam.vn/van-ban/xem-truoc-file-3c746fe0-02bc-a000-70a3-cd7d08f31ea0>

The file remains a secondary-hosted original-scan lead. Download access was not
available without a member account, and sign-up from the United States required
a Vietnamese telephone number. No account or access-control workaround was
attempted.

Official Bình Phước sources independently corroborate the administrative
history:

- the provincial history page lists Decree 14/NĐ-CP in the sequence of Bình
  Phước administrative changes:
  <https://dulich.binhphuoc.gov.vn/vi/history>;
- the provincial news portal states that Decree 14/NĐ-CP was issued on
  2009-04-10 to create xã Quang Minh:
  <https://binhphuoc.gov.vn/vi/news/tin-tuc-su-kien-421/xa-quang-minh-ky-niem-15-nam-thanh-lap-37141.html>.

Classification:

- TVPL letter: `secondary_full_text_related_instrument`;
- LuatVietnam file: `secondary_original_scan_lead_authentication_required`;
- Bình Phước pages: `official_secondary`;
- primary-source status: still open.

## `28/NQ-CP@2009-06-30`

TVPL publishes the complete transcribed text of Resolution 28/NQ-CP:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Nghi-quyet-28-NQ-CP-dieu-chinh-va-mo-rong-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-91273.aspx>

The transcription identifies the Government as issuer, Prime Minister Nguyễn
Tấn Dũng as signatory, and 2009-06-29 as the issue date. Article 2 states that
the resolution took effect on the date of signing. The operative text adjusts
Châu Thành and Chợ Gạo district boundaries to expand Mỹ Tho and creates or
adjusts communes in Mỹ Tho, Châu Thành, and Chợ Gạo. The official issue and
effective date are therefore one day before the pinned NSO index date.

An official Mỹ Tho government planning PDF independently cites Resolution
28/NQ-CP by code, date, and boundary-expansion purpose:

<https://mytho.tiengiang.gov.vn/documents/20121/457463/7.%2B%C4%90%E1%BB%81%2B%C3%A1n%2Bg%E1%BB%ADi%2Bt%E1%BB%89nh.pdf/840cff0e-1f2b-94ab-4366-f1de47655737?t=1709717424414>

TVPL advertises an original/PDF download, but access requires a paid member
account. Sign-up from the United States required a Vietnamese telephone number,
so the original file was not retrieved and no access-control workaround was
attempted.

Classification:

- TVPL page: `secondary_full_text`;
- Mỹ Tho planning PDF: `official_secondary`;
- TVPL original file: `secondary_original_scan_lead_authentication_required`;
- primary-source status: still open.
