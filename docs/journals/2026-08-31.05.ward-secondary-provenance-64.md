# Ward official lead and secondary provenance: Decree 64/2006/NĐ-CP

Date: 2026-08-31

The pinned NSO instrument `64/2006/NĐ-CP@2006-07-08` now has two complete
commercial legal-database transcriptions, indexed Vietnamese and English
official VBPL records, and official Ministry of Home Affairs systematization
metadata. It remains primary-source-open because no complete official page or
original attachment could be saved, hashed, and verified offline.

## Established identity and legal dates

The reviewed sources identify Decree 64/2006/NĐ-CP as issued by the Government
on 2006-06-23, signed by Prime Minister Phan Văn Khải, and titled:

> Về việc điều chỉnh địa giới hành chính xã, phường của thị xã Hà Giang và
> huyện Vị Xuyên, tỉnh Hà Giang

The three relevant dates remain distinct:

- official issue date: `2006-06-23`;
- official effective date: `2006-07-16`; and
- pinned NSO registry/observation date: `2006-07-08`.

Article 2 makes the decree effective 15 days after Gazette publication. The
official VBPL indexes and Ministry of Home Affairs systematization give
2006-07-16 as the legal effective date. The NSO snapshot observes the parent
changes eight days earlier. Its date remains an observation key and is not
silently rewritten.

## Enacted operations and ward-level topology

Article 1 transfers five whole communes between thị xã Hà Giang and huyện Vị
Xuyên without changing their names or codes:

| Commune | Code | From | To | Transferred total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phú Linh | `00706` | thị xã Hà Giang | huyện Vị Xuyên | 4,350 ha; 5,231 people |
| Kim Thạch | `00703` | thị xã Hà Giang | huyện Vị Xuyên | 3,050 ha; 2,353 people |
| Kim Linh | `00709` | thị xã Hà Giang | huyện Vị Xuyên | 3,590 ha; 2,529 people |
| Phương Độ | `00946` | huyện Vị Xuyên | thị xã Hà Giang | 4,303 ha; 3,515 people |
| Phương Thiện | `00949` | huyện Vị Xuyên | thị xã Hà Giang | 3,219 ha; 3,227 people |

The decree also transfers 103.4 ha and 77 people from Phường Quang Trung in
thị xã Hà Giang to Xã Phong Quang in huyện Vị Xuyên. Afterward:

- Phường Quang Trung has 1,005.6 ha and 3,605 people;
- Xã Phong Quang has 3,478.4 ha and 1,855 people;
- thị xã Hà Giang has 13,025.6 ha, 40,058 people, and eight commune-level
  units; and
- huyện Vị Xuyên has 148,750.4 ha, 91,825 people, and 24 commune-level units.

The SOAP interval `soap:2006-07-04->2006-07-08` observes the five whole-commune
transfers as same-code district-parent changes. This agrees exactly with
crosswalk rows 295–297 and 377–378 of
`crosswalk/ward_2006-01-01_2007-01-01.xls`, each of which retains the unit code
and cites Decree 64. The 103.4-hectare Quang Trung-to-Phong Quang transfer is
roster-invisible because both units retain their names, codes, types, and
district parents. The legal text supplies that boundary lineage beyond the
snapshot delta.

This is a `parent_or_boundary_only` instrument: it creates or dissolves no
commune-level identity, but it changes the parent spans of five continuous
units and the boundary extents of two more.

The raw SOAP rows label district code `024` as `Thành phố Hà Giang`, a later
parent-name echo. The decree consistently uses `thị xã Hà Giang`, its legal
status in 2006. The raw observation is preserved, but the later parent label is
not projected backward as legal history.

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=15920>

The official English index independently renders the same instrument as
Decree 64/2006/NĐ-CP on adjustment of the Hà Giang and Vị Xuyên boundaries.
The Ministry of Home Affairs' official 2024 systematization volume also lists
the same code, title, 2006-06-23 issue date, and 2006-07-16 effective date:

<https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/HHTDanhMuc/Attachments/633/Tap%20HTH%202024.pdf>

These official indexed representations establish identity and dates, but they
do not satisfy the raw-artifact gate. Direct requests to the full-text, print,
Ministry of Home Affairs mirror, and properties endpoints returned HTTP 403
challenge pages. The indexed output did not expose an exact attachment
filename, so no filename is guessed or registered. The legacy
`vbpl.moj.gov.vn` hostname failed DNS resolution.

The Government legal portal returned HTTP 502 during bounded exact-code and
title retries, and web searches found no alternate Government portal record.
A year-bounded Government Gazette API search returned no original publication
PDF.

Classification: `official_full_text_lead`, with raw retrieval blocked. Primary
closure still requires saving the complete official page or an attachment it
actually exposes, recording its source metadata and hash, and verifying its
identity and contents offline.
