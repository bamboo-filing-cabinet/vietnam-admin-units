# Ward secondary provenance: Resolution 935/NQ-UBTVQH13

Date: 2026-08-29

The pinned NSO instrument `935/NQ-UBTVQH13@2015-05-16` remains
primary-source-open. The supplied sources establish strong official derivative
provenance across all three administrative changes in the resolution, but they
do not supply its complete enacted text.

## Official provincial implementation letter

Thanh Hóa's official legal portal registers provincial Letter 5505/UBND-CN,
dated 2015-06-11:

<https://qppl.thanhhoa.gov.vn/vbpq_thanhhoa.nsf/str/89A293B21BDB2C5A47257E620003D397>

The portal also provides the one-page signed and stamped scan:

<https://qppl.thanhhoa.gov.vn/vbpq_thanhhoa.nsf/str/89A293B21BDB2C5A47257E620003D397/$file/c5505.pdf>

The letter identifies Resolution 935/NQ-UBTVQH13 and its 2015-05-15 date and
reproduces the complete subject at branch level:

1. adjust Quảng Xương district and Sầm Sơn town to expand Sầm Sơn town;
2. adjust three communes and one township in Nông Cống district to expand
   Nông Cống township; and
3. adjust three communes and one township in Đông Sơn district to expand Rừng
   Thông township.

It transmits Vice Chair Nguyễn Ngọc Hồi's direction to the Department of Home
Affairs, Department of Construction, Department of Natural Resources and
Environment, Sầm Sơn town, and the Đông Sơn, Nông Cống, and Quảng Xương
districts to implement the resolution urgently. The signed scan records Chief
of Office Ngô Hoàng Kỳ as signatory and says that the resolution was enclosed,
but the portal's downloadable PDF contains only the implementation letter.

Classification: `official_downstream_implementation_original`. This is an
official original artifact and direct provincial implementation provenance for
the target's exact code, date, title, scope, and responsible local governments.
It is primary evidence for Letter 5505 but remains derivative evidence for
Resolution 935; it does not contain the target's operative figures, clauses,
signature, or effective-date provision.

## General Statistics Office implementation table

TVPL publishes a complete transcription of General Statistics Office Letter
546/TCTK-PPCĐ dated 2015-07-30:

<https://thuvienphapluat.vn/cong-van/Bo-may-hanh-chinh/Cong-van-546-TCTK-PPCD-2015-ma-so-don-vi-hanh-chinh-moi-284849.aspx>

Its Thanh Hóa table associates Resolution 935/NQ-UBTVQH13 with 2015-05-15 and
records the code-bearing results:

- Quảng Minh, Quảng Hùng, Quảng Thọ, Quảng Châu, Quảng Vinh, and Quảng Đại
  communes moved from Quảng Xương district to Sầm Sơn town;
- commune code 16312 for Minh Thọ closed because Minh Thọ was merged into
  Nông Cống township; and
- commune code 16411 for Đông Xuân closed because Đông Xuân was merged into
  Rừng Thông township.

Classification: `secondary_official_document_transcription`. The underlying
official statistical implementation letter strongly verifies the resolution's
date and code-level topology consequences. The accessible copy is on a
commercial legal host, and the table does not capture the target's partial
boundary transfers or complete legal clauses.

## Rừng Thông implementation ceremony

A Thanh Hóa health-network page reports Đông Sơn district's 2015-09-16
ceremony announcing Resolution 935:

<http://ytequanson.ytethanhhoa.gov.vn/web/trang-chu/tin-hoat-dong/tin-hoat-dong-cua-don-vi/le-cong-bo-nghi-quyet-so-935-nq-ubtvqh13-cua-uy-ban-thuong-vu-quoc-hoi-ve-viec-dieu-chinh-mo-rong-dia-gioi-hanh-chinh-thi-tran-rung-thong.html>

It describes the Rừng Thông branch in detail: all 190.67 hectares and 2,808
residents of Đông Xuân commune; 93.07 hectares and 1,970 residents of Toàn Tân
village; 181.04 hectares and 2,615 residents of Đại Đồng village; 17.26
hectares of agricultural land in Triệu Xá 1 village of Đông Tiến commune; and
31.05 hectares and 50 residents from villages 6 and 7 of Đông Anh commune. It
reports the resulting Rừng Thông township as 603.16 hectares with 10,878
residents and 12 villages or urban blocks, and records the implementation and
handover instructions.

The URL is served from the Quan Sơn health subdomain even though the report
concerns Đông Sơn. Its content and Thanh Hóa health-network hosting are useful,
but the shared or misassigned subdomain lowers confidence in its archival
identity.

Classification: `official_local_implementation_press`. It is strong
contemporary corroboration for the Rừng Thông branch, not the complete enacted
resolution.

## Later Sầm Sơn corroboration

Báo Lao Động's 2022 report quotes Thanh Hóa Provincial People's Committee
Chair Đỗ Minh Tuấn while reviewing Sầm Sơn's administrative development:

<https://laodong.vn/dttg/dai-doan-ket-dan-toc/khoi-cong-khu-luu-niem-can-bo-mien-nam-tap-ket-ra-bac-1086331.html>

The report identifies Resolution 935/NQ-UBTVQH13, the 2015-05-15 date, and the
transfer of six intact Quảng Xương communes into Sầm Sơn town. It then
distinguishes the later 2017 Resolution 368/NQ-UBTVQH14 that established Sầm
Sơn city.

Classification: `secondary_retrospective_official_statement`. It provides
useful later corroboration for the Sầm Sơn branch but does not name the six
communes or cover the Nông Cống and Rừng Thông branches.

## Coverage and remaining gaps

Taken together, the official provincial letter and statistical table establish
the resolution's exact identity and all three branches. The local ceremony
report adds detailed Rừng Thông figures, while the later Lao Động report
corroborates the Sầm Sơn transfer. The remaining evidentiary weakness is most
pronounced for Nông Cống: the sources identify the branch and Minh Thọ merger,
but do not provide all partial-transfer figures or the final township totals.

No supplied source contains the complete target resolution, its recitals, all
operative boundary figures, resulting-unit clauses, implementation clause,
signature block, or effective-date provision.

## Date discrepancy retained

The pinned NSO instrument key uses `2015-05-16`, the first relevant observed
snapshot date. The official provincial implementation letter and General
Statistics Office table identify Resolution 935 as dated `2015-05-15`. Without
the complete enacted resolution and its effective-date clause, this review
records the discrepancy without changing the observation-based key or
inferring the legal effective date.
